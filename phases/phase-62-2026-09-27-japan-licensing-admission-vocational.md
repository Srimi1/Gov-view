## Medical recognition: foreign education and Japanese requirements

[MHLW's foreign-graduate route](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/stf/newpage_56664.html) requires individual recognition before national medical examination entry; a foreign school cannot promise blanket recognition. Its current page gives **27 February 2026** recognition-sheet submission deadline, without a cutoff clock time. This is an elapsed 2026 intake, not a newly open 2027 application.

The retained [current criteria table](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/content/10800000/001448827.png) was checked visually. It conditionally requires **JLPT N1**, including the explicitly recognised old Level 1 category used through December 2009, for applicants without the specified Japanese junior/high-school education. Exact schooling exception and certificate proof require original-language review. Educational duration/hours, graduation recency, school recognition and foreign licensing criteria also differ between direct and preliminary routes.

[Clinical Japanese assessment](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/stf/newpage_50884.html) is separate: clinical listening, speaking, reading, writing and examination communication are evaluated. Native language certification alone cannot establish eligibility. The [retained 2024 clarification](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/topics/2012/05/dl/tp0525-01_09.pdf) excludes traditional-medicine-only education/licensing. This licensing pathway does not establish a public job, appointment tenure or immigration/work permission. Jinji's recruitment nationality restriction must not be copied to it.

## MEXT undergraduate admission: current bilingual edition

[2027 embassy-recommendation index](https://www.studyinjapan.go.jp/en/smap-stopj-applications-undergraduate.html) links current Japanese and English guidelines. Country-specific embassy schedules differ; general April/May recruitment guidance is not a precise worldwide application window.

Retained [English](https://www.studyinjapan.go.jp/en/_mt/2026/04/2027_Guidelines_Undergraduate_E.pdf) and [Japanese originals](https://www.studyinjapan.go.jp/en/_mt/2026/04/2027_Guidelines_Undergraduate_J.pdf) describe eligible international students from diplomatic-partner countries and special conditions for Japanese dual nationals. General application requires willingness to learn and study in Japanese, without a universal JLPT/CEFR threshold. Direct-placement universities can require language tests or interviews. Certificate submission is conditional when existing language credentials are held; it is not a universal N1 prerequisite.

Scholarship application, screening, preparatory study, placement and university admission are distinct. Preparation does not guarantee admission. No application was sent and no profile or identity document was used. India embassy notice access remains a separate known gap.

## Vocational qualifications: grade is not language proficiency

[MHLW's national skills framework](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/stf/seisakunitsuite/bunya/koyou_roudou/jinzaikaihatsu/ability_skill/ginoukentei/index.html) covers prefectural and designated-body examinations. Its trade inventory does not count applications, vacancies or venues. A qualification does not promise employment or a visa.

[Foreign-trainee guidance](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/stf/seisakunitsuite/bunya/koyou_roudou/shokugyounouryoku/ability_skill/ginoukentei/kisokyu.html) separates basic, ad-hoc grade 3 and ad-hoc grade 2 routes. These are vocational grades, not language levels. Retained 2021 overview describes Japanese exam medium and route-specific reading accommodations, without a universal JLPT/CEFR threshold. Linked leaflet is marked March 2025. Both are framework evidence, not new 2026 intake notices; trade, grade, trainee-plan status and actual examining-body conditions still require review.
