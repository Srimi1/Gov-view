# Phase 43 — Six worldwide public recruitment portals

Date: 2026-09-25. Saved in Gov-view folder. Research-only registry expansion; no application cycles published.

## Official authorities checked

| Jurisdiction | Official entry point | What source establishes | Collection gap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Germany | [Federal administration job search](https://karriere.bund.de/arbeiten-bei-uns/stellenangebote-bei-uns) | Federal employment, civil-service and training search; notice cards show deadlines and job types. | Individual notices, foreign-applicant rules, states and local authorities. |
| Spain | [Public Access Point](https://administracion.gob.es/en/empleopublico) | Government links to call search, weekly bulletin and selection-test registration. | Original calls, autonomous communities and role-level rules. |
| Italy | [inPA](https://www.inpa.gov.it/) | Public-administration notice search and official gazette links. | Individual bandi, amendments, role-level nationality and language rules. |
| Republic of Korea | [National civil-service system](https://gongmuwon.gosi.kr/rccom/ComBbNtcMttrLst.do) | Current national recruitment domain. | Direct page sometimes times out; original Korean notices and regional authorities. |
| Philippines | [Civil Service Commission job portal](https://csc.gov.ph/career/) | Portal advisory addresses qualified Filipino citizens and directs applications to each agency. | Dynamic job rows and authoritative agency notices. |
| Nigeria | [Federal Civil Service recruitment portal](https://recruitment.fedcivilservice.gov.ng/) | Official portal leads to vacancies page. | Vacancy rows unavailable to this collector; no current opening verified. |

## Registry and eligibility treatment

- Added six entries to `sources/registry.json`, each with `connector: none` and `enabled: false`. Registered country reach is now **23 of 250**; registration means identified authority, not audited coverage or verified current listings.
- No deadline, job type, citizenship/residence, language proficiency or foreign-citizen eligibility was inferred for an individual cycle. Philippines' portal-level Filipino-citizen advisory remains separate from each hiring agency's rules. Korean page language is not a language requirement.
- These portals cover public recruitment only. Licensing, admission and vocational authorities remain separate inventory work in each jurisdiction.
