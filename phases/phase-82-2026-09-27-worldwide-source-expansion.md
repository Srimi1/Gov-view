## India priority: RBI

The [official vacancy directory](https://opportunities.rbi.org.in/Scripts/Vacancies.aspx) rendered current contract medical-consultant roles and extension notices in the ordinary browser. The complete directory has not been reconciled. Extensions are revisions, not automatically new application cycles.

[Grade B original advertisement](https://opportunities.rbi.org.in/Scripts/bs_viewcontent.aspx?Id=4997), dated 29 April 2026, is panel year 2026. It describes direct recruitment on probation, initially two years and extendable to four. Its original application deadline was 20 May at 18:00; the governing timezone remains unverified. This elapsed window must not become an open intake.

Nationality rules include Indian citizens and specified Nepal/Bhutan, Tibetan-refugee and Indian-origin migration categories. Required Government of India eligibility certificate may be pending at examination admission, but appointment offer requires issuance. This distinction does not clear age, education, selection or other conditions. No universal foreign-applicant permission inferred.

[Assistant advertisement](https://opportunities.rbi.org.in/scripts/bs_viewcontent.aspx?Id=4912) is **panel year 2025**, advertised in February 2026. Its selection requires English examination sections and a mandatory state official/local-language proficiency test, without exemption. No CEFR equivalent is assigned. The office-language appendix and linked corrigendum/April notices remain unreviewed. Grade B language appendices are separate; Assistant criteria cannot be transferred to them.

Reader access returned human-verification responses for RBI HTML and one appendix; other appendices were inaccessible. Ordinary browser rendered the HTML. No CAPTCHA solved or PDF original retained. Exam-city lists establish neither residence scope nor actual venue coordinates; online examination does not automatically mean remote-from-home.

### Samoa circular interpretation

Current [PSC homepage](https://www.psc.gov.ws/) links [issue 36/2026, dated 22 September](https://www.psc.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/2026_36.pdf), rather than cached search issue 32. Circular separates contract, permanent and term vacancies from provisional/confirmed appointments and appeals. An appeal deadline is not a recruitment window. Re-advertisements distinguish whether prior applicants must reapply.

Children, Youth & Family Development reference is `WS002600` in the circular versus `FA002600` in the contract table: unresolved conflict, no automatic intake merge. PDF text ordering needs visual reconciliation. AG003269 ZIP returned unsupported content type; it was not downloaded or extracted. Template timestamps do not replace notice dates.
