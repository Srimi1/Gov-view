### Language evidence retained without invented equivalence

Malaysia's [N1 appointment conditions](https://www.spa.gov.my/syarat-permohonan/spm-svm/pembantu-tadbir-perkeranian-operasi-gred-n1) require a Bahasa Melayu credit, at least **Grade C at SPM/SVM**, or a government-recognized equivalent. This is a published school-subject qualification, not CEFR and not a language requirement inferred from the Malay interface. It has not been converted into an automatic profile check or generalized to every Malaysian opportunity. Native evidence and qualification-equivalence review remain necessary.

Belgium's retained language page describes administrative, judicial and selection-purpose language tests, certificates and exemptions. No universal CEFR level is derived for all federal vacancies. Test frameworks, diploma-based exemptions, exact appointment requirements and the stage at which proof is needed require original notice-level review.

For all eight entries, appointment kind, ability to apply, eligibility to enter selection and eligibility for the resulting job/licence remain separate assessments. Published Malaysia SPA9 citizenship and N1 language findings are research facts; no source-level finding is automatically applied to unrelated jobs, pathways or authorities. Missing language evidence stays unknown.
