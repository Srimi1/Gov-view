[Nationality FAQ](https://www.pref.osaka.lg.jp/faq/o210010/faq_001643.html), updated 17 March 2026, permits non-Japanese competitive-exam candidates except police administration, with appointment restrictions.

[Current guard/civil-construction guide](https://www.pref.osaka.lg.jp/o040030/jinji/senkou/r8_ginou.html), updated 28 August 2026, separately confirms foreign-citizen access and residence status permitting the duties at appointment. Birth on/after 2 April 1977 is required; education/work experience are not required. Original window is 28 August 10:00–1 October 18:00, 2026. Source does not establish an official timezone.
