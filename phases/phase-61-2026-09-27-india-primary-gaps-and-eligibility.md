## Haryana: conditional foreign routes and training stage

The already retained [HPSC Food Safety Officer advertisement 25/2026](https://hpsc.gov.in/Portals/0/Advt_25_2026_FSO_18_09_2026.pdf) still matches its recorded SHA-256. All 30 scanned pages were OCR-processed; pages 4, 5 and 16 were checked visually against saved images. OCR remains provisional research.

- Page 16 permits Indian citizens and specified Nepal/Bhutan, Tibetan-refugee and Indian-origin migrant categories. Non-Indian categories need a Government of India eligibility certificate. Foreign nationality alone cannot establish a match.
- Page 5 requires relevant certificates/documents by closing and rejects documents issued later. Do not assume an appointment-stage certificate grace period; founder/authority confirmation remains necessary.
- Page 4 requires Hindi/Sanskrit as a Matric subject, or Hindi in higher education. This is an academic-subject condition, without a CEFR level.
- The same page says food-authority training is provided after appointment. An extraction must not automatically reject an otherwise eligible applicant for lacking this future training.
- Reservation domicile evidence is distinct from nationality. Group B and pay scale do not prove permanent tenure; an experience-certificate template listing prior employment types cannot establish this job's appointment arrangement.

The prior publication-date conflict and current amendment-access gap remain unresolved. Research is not human approval or a current verified application listing. Updated [research record](../data/evidence/research/hpsc-25-2026-research.json) preserves the previous incomplete nationality finding and this follow-up.
