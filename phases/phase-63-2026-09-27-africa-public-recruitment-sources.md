# Phase 63 — African public recruitment sources

Saved 27 September 2026, Asia/Kolkata. Previous phase expanded Japan's pathways. This phase adds ten official discovery sources in ten previously unregistered jurisdictions. All remain disabled, without notice adapters, and require review.

## Sources and eligibility boundaries

| Jurisdiction | Official source | Research finding and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ghana | [PSC vacancies](https://psc.gov.gh/job-vacancies/), [FAQ](https://psc.gov.gh/faqs/) | Retained FAQ gives Ghanaian nationality and age at least 18 as general recruitment conditions. Recruitment below director level is decentralized; original public-service organization notices remain necessary. No universal language level or appointment tenure established. |
| Tanzania | [PSRS](https://www.ajira.go.tz/), [mandate](https://www.ajira.go.tz/whoarewe) | Retained mandate page links agency, university and council advertisements. Recruitment, volunteering, interview results and placements differ. Swahili notice language does not establish applicant proficiency. Linked current PDF originals remain unretained. Zanzibar and other authorities require separate coverage. |
| Uganda | [PSC portal](https://vacancies.psc.go.ug/), [ministry referral](https://www.publicservice.go.ug/) | Retained ministry explicitly links this portal and legacy `/ors`. Indexed internal staff notices cannot become general-public applications. Current nationality, language, appointment arrangements and original notices remain unverified. |
| Rwanda | [MIFOTRA recruitment](https://www.recruitment.mifotra.gov.rw/), [ministry explanation](https://www.mifotra.gov.rw/news-detail/recruitment-of-public-servants-digitized-in-rwanda) | Official ministry identifies the portal and diaspora access. Diaspora does not imply foreign nationality permission. Mandatory profile language fields do not establish a per-job proficiency threshold. Retained portal is a JavaScript shell. |
| Zambia | [Civil Service Commission](https://www.csc.gov.zm/) | Official reader establishes authority mandate, excluding constitutional office holders. No usable current notice register or applicant criteria verified; original bytes unavailable. Other commissions remain gaps. |
| Zimbabwe | [PSC e-recruitment](https://erecruitment.psc.gov.zw/), [authority](https://psc.gov.zw/) | Retained authority confirms portal backlink. [ICT deputy-director detail 216](https://erecruitment.psc.gov.zw/jobs/216) gives 5 October 2026 deadline and Full-time label. Full-time is work pattern, not permanent tenure. Harare is work location, not an exam venue. Nationality/language conditions remain unresolved. |
| Botswana | [Government careers](https://gov.bw/careers) | Retained page says no current job opportunities on that page. This is not an audited country-wide empty state. Other ministries, employers and pathway sources remain gaps. |
| Namibia | [OPM](https://opm.gov.na/) | Official indexed circulars establish public-service vacancy authority. Historical citizen preference is not a foreign-citizen ban. Current circular index, original bytes and exact per-job eligibility need review. |
| Mauritius | [PSC/DFSC](https://psc.govmu.org/psc/) | Official reader says these commissions accept only citizens. Public/internal and online/manual routes differ; Rodrigues posts are present. This advisory cannot establish every public contract's eligibility. Original bytes and per-post language/academic criteria unavailable. |
| Seychelles | [Public Service Bureau](https://www.psb.gov.sc/vacancies), [government directory](https://www.gov.sc/departments) | Bureau page retained and says no vacancies currently listed. This does not establish absence of hiring elsewhere. Nationality, natural-language criteria and appointment type remain unknown. |

English, French, Swahili, Kinyarwanda or Creole interface/notice language never becomes an inferred CEFR level. Citizen restrictions, citizen preferences, residence, qualification equivalence and internal-staff eligibility remain separate conditions. Generic website descriptions of career stability cannot establish a permanent appointment.
