### Federated States of Micronesia — National Government

The [Personnel directory](https://personnel.gov.fm/fsm-government-vacancies/) separates national vacancies, consultancies, other entities and bidding. [Financial Management Specialist FSM-073-26](https://personnel.gov.fm/financial-management-specialist-fsm-department-of-finance-administration-pohnpei-closing-date-10-25-2026/) opens 24 September and closes 25 October 2026, date only. It prioritizes qualified FSM citizens, with other Micronesians and US citizens considered where no qualified FSM citizen is available. This conditional route requires definition and stage review. A relevant bachelor's degree and three years financial-management experience remain independent criteria. Tenure, formal language level, cutoff time and timezone are unknown. Four state governments and directory pagination remain coverage gaps. Palikir is duty location, not exam venue. The page's Image link led to a government logo, not another original notice.
