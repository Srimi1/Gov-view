## Eligibility and language findings

CIR page requires Indian passport and degree by July 2026. Placements list Japanese N1 for Hidaka, Hamamatsu and Matsue; Toyama lists N2 or above. Hidaka specifies English and Hindi plus driving licence; Toyama specifies English and Telugu; others specify English. Rajasthan/Bihar, Andhra Pradesh and Kerala origins are preferences, not compulsory residence restrictions. General page permits Japanese N2 or NAT level 3; these frameworks cannot be automatically converted or collapsed into one mandatory certificate.

[Linked CIR guidelines](https://www.in.emb-japan.go.jp/files/100962323.pdf) instead describe functional Japanese equivalent to N1/N2 and English/Hindi/Telugu alternatives. They exclude recent JET participation from **2023**, whereas webpage says **2022**. Differences need authority reconciliation before deterministic rules or approval. One-year appointment terms and contracting-organisation assignment remain distinct from permanent employment guarantees.

ALT webpage requires strong English, with Japanese background receiving additional consideration. Its stated deadline remains 30 October 2026, but [linked 2027 guidelines](https://www.in.emb-japan.go.jp/files/100720531.pdf) say 31 October and allow a school-teaching credential alternative absent from webpage's bachelor requirement. Existing ALT research was refreshed with this qualification discrepancy. No date or education rule is silently chosen. ALT placements can include private schools; programme administration does not make every placement a government job.
