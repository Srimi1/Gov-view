### Tonga — PSC

The [official directory](https://psc.gov.to/index.php/all-vacancies/) mixes vacancies, extensions and procurement. A [September extension](https://psc.gov.to/index.php/2026/09/04/re-advertisement-of-ten-10-vacant-permanent-positions-at-the-office-of-the-public-service-commission-2/) covers ten permanent posts and closed 11 September 2026 at 16:30, timezone unknown. Its linked four-page document was readable through the site's public Google viewer. Candidates within and outside public service are invited; external recruitment does not prove foreign-citizen permission. Serving public servants need CEO endorsement. Successful non-permanent entrants need medical and specified residence-country police evidence for appointment. Nationality and formal language level remain unverified. Procurement invitations are excluded from recruitment; extensions are revisions rather than new intakes.
