# GOV View

GOV View describes public career pathways from official evidence without treating uncertain details as settled.

## Language

**Application cycle**:
One distinct application or registration intake for a recruitment, licence, admission, or vocational pathway. Multiple places or examination venues within that intake do not create more cycles.

**Official deadline time zone**:
The civil time zone governing an application deadline when the source establishes it. It remains unknown when a date is published without a governing zone, especially for programmes spanning several zones.

**Cutoff precision**:
The level of detail the authority gives for a deadline: date, minute, second, or unknown. A published date alone does not establish a closing clock time.

**Foreign applicant eligibility**:
Whether a person who is not a citizen of the hiring jurisdiction can apply, enter selection, and obtain the resulting job or credential. A preference for citizens does not, by itself, establish a ban on foreign applicants.
