## Source-format warning

All-jobs table prints issue-date header “MM/DD/YYYY,” but includes `31/08/2026` and `24/08/2026`. Those rows establish day-first order for this snapshot. Raw strings and parsed dates are both retained in `in-employment-news.json`; future snapshots stop if this inference cannot be established. Last-date header says DD/MM/YYYY. Neither column is an official hiring-authority deadline until checked against original notice.
