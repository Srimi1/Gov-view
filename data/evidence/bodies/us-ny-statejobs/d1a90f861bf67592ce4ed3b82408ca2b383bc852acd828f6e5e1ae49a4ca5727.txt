				<p class="row"><span class="leftCol"><a   href="/assets/help/vacancyReviewHelp.cfm#vacancyInfo.duties" title="The duties that the incumbent of the vacancy will be expected to perform." class="help"></a> Duties Description </span><span class="rightCol">The New York State Education Department’s (NYSED) Office of Education Policy is seeking candidates for an Associate in Instructional Services (English Language Arts 7-12) in the Office of Standards and Instruction. Duties of this position include, but are not limited to, the following:<br /><br />•	Provide technical assistance to school personnel, BOCES, districts, parents, and other stakeholders on P-12 English Language Arts instruction, the English Language Arts Learning Standards, P-12 instructional programs, literacy, NY Inspires, and other NYSED priorities;<br />•	Conduct research, synthesize and analyze quantitative and qualitative data, and write reports, grants, contracts, proposed regulatory and policy statements, guidance documents, and various informational materials;<br />•	Provide expertise on instructional design, instructional practice, curricular materials, and assessment models in English Language Arts and interdisciplinary connections at the elementary, middle, and secondary levels;<br />•	Develop and facilitate virtual and in-person presentations to a variety of stakeholders to provide information, guidance, and support on topics including the NYS Learning Standards for English Language Arts, literacy education, Office programs, NY Inspires, and other NYSED priorities;<br />•	Administer a variety of programs to support P-12 teaching and learning, including but not limited to competitive federal and State grant programs, contracts, and award programs, to ensure deliverables are on time and of high quality;<br />•	Communicate and collaborate with other NYSED offices, state professional organizations, and other stakeholder groups to provide information, guidance, support, and resources related to English Language Arts, literacy, and other NYSED priorities; and<br />•	Perform additional duties as assigned by Supervisor.</span> </p>
