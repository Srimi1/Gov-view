				<p class="row"><span class="leftCol"><a   href="/assets/help/vacancyReviewHelp.cfm#vacancyInfo.duties" title="The duties that the incumbent of the vacancy will be expected to perform." class="help"></a> Duties Description </span><span class="rightCol">DISTINGUISHING FEATURES OF WORK: Principal Attorneys serve in a confidential capacity in the Mental Hygiene Legal Services, attorney disciplinary committees, departmental law-guardian offices, or other such units where they research legal questions and issues, organize complex investigations, prepare and present complex cases before administrative tribunals and trial and appellate courts, and perform related duties. Princi al Attorneys may also assist in selecting and training new staff, developing and implementing policies and procedures and in handling day-to-day administrative matters. They may also work with advisory committees, assist with planning and executing educational programs, assist with resolving administrative problems, and serve as director of a departmental law guardian office.<br /><br />ASSIGNMENT: Principal Attorneys handle a substantial caseload and support the Principal Attorney-in-Charge in supervising an MHLS field office and may oversee daily operations in MHLS field offices, including monitoring time and attendance, procuring goods and services, maintaining statistical records and interfacing with building management on facility-related issues. Principal Attorneys may participate in the structured interview process for prospective employees, provide training and skills development, prepare annual performance evaluations and staff development  plans, and provide oversight and coverage for other Principal Attorneys who are unavailable or on leave. ln addition, they may serve as subject matter experts and lead a team of attorneys in a particular area of the Mental Hygiene Law or a specific legal mandate of the agency. Excellent lawyering, interpersonal and communication skills are required. A valid driver’s license and car are required.</span> </p>
