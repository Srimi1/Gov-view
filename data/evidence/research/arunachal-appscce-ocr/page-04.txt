C. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA

In pursuant to Government Notification issued vide F.No.AR-33/17/2026-SECT-1-AR (Computer
No.326793) /111 dated 26"" August, 2026, all the candidates applying for Arunachal Pradesh Public
Service Combined Competitive Examination-2026 must satisfy the following conditions of
Mandatory Eligibility Criteria:

(1) Permanent Residence Criterion:

The candidate must be a citizen of India and an inhabitant of Arunachal Pradesh holding a
valid Permanent Residence Certificate (PRC) of Arunachal Pradesh issued by the designated
Competent Authority under the Government of Arunachal Pradesh.

(2) Indigenous Status and Arunachal Pradesh Schedule Tribe Criterion:

The candidate must be an original inhabitant of Arunachal Pradesh and holds an Arunachal
Pradesh Schedule Tribe (APST) Certificate issued by the Competent Authority of the
Government of Arunachal Pradesh, as per the list of Tribes notified by the Government of
India in the Constitution (Scheduled Tribes) Order, 1950 from time to time.

(3) Indigenous Tribal Language Proficiency Criterion:

The candidate must be able to speak any of the Indigenous Tribal Languages of Arunachal
Pradesh as per the list of Tribal Language notified by the Government India under the
Constitution of India Scheduled Tribes Order, 1950, as amended from time to time and must
possess working knowledge of the local indigenous tribes. However, the implementation
Mechanism for Language Test will be as per the provision of the Government Notification
issued vide F.No.AR-33/17/2026-SECT-1-AR (Computer No.326793)/111 dated 26" August,
2026.
