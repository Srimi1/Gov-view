K. RESERVATION

(1) The Govt. of A.P. policy of reservation in all Government jobs for APST candidates will be
enforced.

(2) The implementation of Reservation Policy for Persons with Disability will be in accordance with
the requisition submitted by the Department of Personnel and roster maintained by the
requisitioning/indenting Government Departments and also as per the Govt. Notification issued
from time to time.

(3) Person with Benchmark Disability (PwD) candidates should submit disability certificate issued
by the certifying authority as per Section 57 of Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016,
failing which, such candidates will be treated as normal candidate and shall not be entitled for
any concession. In the Medical Board, the Specialist of the particular disabilities, for which
certificate is sought, must be a member. Otherwise, the Disability Certificate will not be
accepted.

L. EXAMINATION FEES

(1) Candidates are required to pay fee of % 200/- (Rupees Two Hundred) only through online
mode.

(2) PwD candidates are exempted from payment of application fee as per Section 19 of the
Arunachal Pradesh Rights of Persons with Disabilities Rules, 2018.

M. DOCUMENTS TO BE SUBMITTED

(1) Online application is now invited for APPSCCE (Preliminary) only. Candidates who qualify
in the APPSCCE (Preliminary) will be required to fill up a Detailed Application Form (DAF)
again for the APPSCCE (Main Examination). The APPSC will issue a notification in this
regard after declaration of the result of the APPSCCE (Preliminary Examination). However,
candidates will not be required to pay any additional application fee for the same.

(2) Scanned copies of following documents and information are to be submitted with online
application form for appscce-2026 (preliminary examination):

a) Signature of the candidate on white paper with black ink pen (40-50 kb in jpg format).

b) _Latest/Recent color passport size photo (40-100 kb in jpg format). The photograph is to be
in white background, front faced and without headgear and sunglasses.

c)  Self-Attested photocopy of Matriculation Certificate/Marksheet with Date of Birth
mentioned issued by the Board for proof of age. No other document shall be accepted as
proof of Date of Birth.

d)  Self-Attested Photocopy of Arunachal Pradesh Scheduled Tribe (APST) certificate issued
by Competent Authority of the Government of Arunachal Pradesh, as per the list of tribes
notified by the Government of India in the Constitution (Scheduled Tribes) Order, 1950, as
amended from time to time.

e)  Self-Attested photocopy of Permanent Residence Certificate (PRC) of Arunachal Pradesh
issued by Competent Authority of the Government of Arunachal Pradesh.

f} The Government Employees of Arunachal Pradesh seeking relaxation in upper age limit
must submit their self-attested photocopy of appointment order (not promotion order) as
proof of continuous regular service of 3 (three) years in the same or allied cadre.
