All candidates in Government service, whether in a permanent or temporary capacity or as
work charged employees, other than casual or daily rated employees or those serving under
Public Enterprises/Banks and other such bodies, shall be required to state in the appropriate
space of the application that they have informed in writing to their Employer/Controlling
authority that they have applied to sit for the examination. Candidate should note that in-case
communication is received from their Employer/Controlling authority by the Commission
withholding permission to the candidate applying for appearing at the examination, their
application shall be rejected and their candidature shall be cancelled.

Applicants in Government Service have to upload a copy of intimation submitted to their
respective Heads of Department or NOC issued by the department at the time of filling the
Detailed Application Form (DAF) on qualifying the Preliminary Stage.

No candidate shall be admitted to the Preliminary Examination unless he/she holds a certificate
of admission (Admit Card) from the Commission for that purpose.

The decision of the Commission as to the eligibility or otherwise of a candidate for admission
to the examination shall be final and binding.

A candidate who indulges in any of the below mentioned unfair means during the examination
or reported thereafter or has been declared by the Commission to be guilty of: -

(i) Obtaining support for his/her candidature by any unfair means, or

(ii) Impersonation, or

(iii) | Procuring impersonation by any person, or

(iv) | Submitted fabricated documents or documents which have been tampered with, or

(vy) Making false or incorrect statement or suppressing material fact or information, or

(vi) Resorting to any other irregular or improper means in connection with his/her
candidature for the examination, or

(vit) Using unfair means during the examination, or

(viii) Writing irrelevant matter, including obscene language or pornographic matter in the
scripts, or

(ix) | Misbehaving in any other manner in the Examination Hall, or

(x) Harassing or doing bodily harm to the Centre Superintendent/Invigilators/Staff
employed by the Commission for the conduct of examination, or

(xi) _ Violating any of the instructions issued to the candidates along with their Admission
Cards / Call Letters permitting them to take the examination, or commission or as the
case may be attempting the commission of all or any of the acts specified in the
foregoing clauses by a candidate may, in addition to rendering him/her liable to
criminal prosecution, also render him/her liable: -

(a) To be disqualified by the Commission from the Examination for which he/she is a
candidate, or

(b) To be debarred permanently or for a specified period: -

1. by the Commission from any examination or selection held by them.
