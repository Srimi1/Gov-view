Person with Disability (PwD)/Person with Benchmark Disability (PwBD) candidates
should submit disability certificate issued by the certifying authority as per Section 57 of
Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016, failing which, such candidates shall not
claim the reservation under PwBD category and shall not be entitled for any concession. In
the Medical Board, the Medical Specialist of the particular disability, for which certificate
is sought, must be a member. The Disability Certificate must clearly mention the
Percentage of Disability. The date of issuance of the Disability Certificate should be
before the last date of submission of online application form. Otherwise, the Disability
Certificate will not be accepted for claiming reservation under PwBD category.

Defence Service Personnel, belonging to APST, disabled in operations during hostility
may submit the relevant document, if availing age relaxation.

The candidature of a candidate at any stage of the examination is provisional and subject to
notification/direction/decision/order/pronouncement of the Government or any Court of
Law. Mere issuance of Admit Card or appearance at the selection process does not entitle
the candidate to any claim for the post.

Candidates falling under the Persons with Benchmark Disability (PwBD) category with 40%
and above percentage of disability must ensure compliance with the functional requirements
prescribed for the post(s) for which the candidate intends to apply. The selection process
will be as per the Right of persons with Disabilities Act, 2016 and Arunachal Pradesh Rights
of Persons with Disabilities Rules, 2018 as amended from time to time.

Eligible candidates will have to produce the above-mentioned (at Point M)
Certificates/Documents and Mark Sheets in ORIGINAL at the time of interview. If a
candidate is found not to be eligible during the document verification prior to the interview,
his/her candidature shall be REJECTED and he/she shall not be allowed to appear in the
interview.

The decision of the Commission as to the eligibility or otherwise of a candidate shall be
final and no correspondence or personal enquiry will be entertained.

The candidate must note that the Appointing Authority, that is, the Indenting/ Requisitioning
Department conducts the medical test and verifies the character and antecedents of the
candidates, authenticity of all the documents/certificates, authenticity of the disability
certificates and eligibility as per the Recruitment Rules before finally appointing the
candidates. Therefore, the provisional recommendation of a candidate does not confer upon
him/her any right to appointment unless the Appointing Authority is satisfied, after such
inquiry as maybe considered necessary, that the candidate is suitable for the appointment to

the post.
