Before filling the Online Application Form for the post advertised, a candidate must
mandatorily register for ONE TIME REGISTRATION (OTR).

Those who have already registered in OTR need not register again and they can directly login
to fill up online form using the same user ID and password.

Candidates are required to apply online on https://appse.gov.in . Detailed instructions for One
Time Registration (OTR) and Online Application are available on the APPSC website

(https://appsc.gov.in).

The applicants are advised to submit only single application, however, if due to any
unavoidable situation, he/she submits another/multiple applications, the application with the
highest Registration ID (RID) will be accepted by the Commission. Therefore, the highest
RID application should be complete in all respects. The fee paid against one RID shall not be
adjusted against any other RID.

The online application submission link shall be active from 1706 hours on 16" September,
2026 till last date of submission i.e. before 1700 hours on 20 October, 2026 on the APPSC
website.

The candidates should carefully fill the requisite details in the online application form.
The application form once submitted within the last date of submission should be
complete in all respects and no additional time shall be provided to the candidate to
further change or upload additional information. The candidates are requested to
minutely go through the DECLARATION provided in the online application form
before final submission of form. Any information found to be wrong or not in
conformity to the conditions laid in the Recruitment Rules or other relevant Govt. Rules
at any stage of examination may lead to cancellation of candidature for APPSCCE-2026.

Applicants should hold a Graduate Degree from any of the University Grants Commission
recognized Universities incorporated by an Act of the Central or State Legislature in India or
other educational institution established by an Act of Parliament or declared to be deemed as a
University under section 3 of the University Grants Commission Act, 1956 or possess such
qualification as may be declared equivalent by the Government.

Applicants who have appeared or who intend to appear at such a qualifying examination shall
also be eligible. However, such candidates will be admitted to the examination, if otherwise
eligible, but their admission shall be deemed to be provisional and subject to cancellation if
they do not produce the proof of having passed the requisite examination, in original, on the
day of Interview/Viva voce test of the Main Examination. If an applicant fails to do so, he/she
will not be permitted to appear in the interview/viva-voce test of the main examination. If the
degree certificate cannot be produced due to non-holding of convocation by the concerned
university, then marksheet issued by competent authority should be produced as proof of
having passed the qualifying examination.
