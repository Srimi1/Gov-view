    Important Information
    • The advertisement also includes backlog vacancies of SC, ST and OBC categories advertised in previous years.
    • Reservation benefits for SC/ST/OBC/EWS/PwBD categories shall be applicable as per Government of India rules and norms.
    • Applicants seeking reservation benefits must upload the necessary documents supporting their category claim.
    • OBC (NCL) certificate must be issued for the current financial year 2026-27, i.e. between 01.04.2026 and the last date of receipt of online applications, and must be valid for Union of India/Central Government jobs.
    • The minimum eligibility conditions shall be as per UGC Regulations, 2018 and subsequent amendments, along with the norms of concerned regulatory bodies wherever applicable.
    • The cut-off date for determining eligibility conditions shall be the last date of receipt of online applications.
    • Applicants who had earlier applied against unfilled teaching positions under Advertisement No. 02/2022/ACD and Advertisement No. 02/2023/ACD are required to apply afresh with updated information. They are exempted from payment of application fee subject to submission of proof of earlier fee payment.
    Eligibility Requirements
    Professor
    • An eminent scholar having a Ph.D. Degree in the concerned/allied/ relevant discipline.
    • Published work of high quality and active engagement in research.
    • Minimum 10 research publications in peer-reviewed or UGC-listed journals.
    • Total research score of 120 as per the prescribed UGC criteria.
    • Minimum 10 years of teaching experience in University/College as Assistant Professor/Associate Professor/Professor and/or research experience at an equivalent level at the University/National level institutions.
    • Evidence of having successfully guided doctoral candidates.
    • An alternative eligibility route is provided for outstanding professionals having a Ph.D. degree in the relevant/allied/applied disciplines, significant contribution to knowledge and ten years' experience, supported by documentary evidence.
    • Specific eligibility conditions apply to Health Science and Nursing disciplines as prescribed in the detailed eligibility document.
    Associate Professor
    • A good academic record with a Ph.D. Degree in the concerned/allied/relevant discipline.
    • Master's degree with at least 55% marks or an equivalent grade.
    • Minimum eight years of teaching and/or research experience in an academic/research position equivalent to Assistant Professor.
    • Minimum seven publications in peer-reviewed or UGC-listed journals.
    • Total research score of 75 as per the prescribed UGC criteria.
    • Specific eligibility conditions apply to Health Science and Nursing disciplines as prescribed in the detailed eligibility document.
    Assistant Professor
    • Master's degree with minimum 55% marks or an equivalent grade in the concerned/relevant/allied subject.
    • The candidate must have cleared the UGC/CSIR National Eligibility Test (NET), or possess a qualifying Ph.D. as per the applicable UGC regulations and amendments.
    • NET shall not be required for Master's programmes/disciplines for which NET is not conducted by UGC/CSIR.
    • Candidates with Ph.D. degrees covered under the applicable UGC provisions may be exempted from the NET requirement.
    • Specific eligibility conditions apply to Health Science and other disciplines as prescribed in the detailed eligibility document.
    Other Eligibility Conditions
    • A minimum of 55% marks, or otherwise specified, at the Master's level shall be the essential qualification for direct recruitment of teachers and equivalent cadres.
    • Relaxation in marks shall be applicable as prescribed under the applicable regulations.
    • Reservation for SC, ST, EWS, OBC (Non-Creamy Layer) and PwBD categories shall be applicable as per UGC/Central Government norms.
    • Applicants claiming reservation benefits must upload valid supporting certificates in the prescribed format.
    • The time taken by candidates to acquire M.Phil. and/or Ph.D. shall not be considered as teaching/research experience, subject to the provisions specified in the detailed eligibility document.
    • Mere possession of the prescribed qualifications and experience does not necessarily entitle an applicant to be called for interview. Applications shall be screened and shortlisted as per the prescribed criteria.
    • The University reserves the right to amend or modify eligibility conditions, number of posts or selection criteria. Any corrigendum/addendum shall be published on the University website.
