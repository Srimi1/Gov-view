Advisory for Candidates

(Regarding Upgraded Version of Recruitment Process Application, RPA Version 3.0)

It is hereby informed for the information of all concerned that the Sikkim Public Service Commission (SPSC) is in the process of upgrading its online recruitment portal to the Recruitment Process Application (RPA) Version 3.0 accessible at spscrecruitment.sikkim.gov.in


  1. In view of the upgraded system, all candidates intending to apply for posts advertised by the Commission, are required to complete a one-time registration (OTR) on the RPA 3.0 portal, by following the instructions and user guidelines made available therein.
  2. In order to access and link their previous applications and records in the upgraded system, candidates who have previously registered in the earlier version of the SPSC recruitment portal are advised to use the same email ID that was used during their earlier registration. This will ensure seamless migration of data and avoid duplication of candidate profiles.
  3. All applicants are requested to carefully read the instructions available on the portal and complete the one-time registration before proceeding to submit online applications for any recruitment advertised by the Commission.
  4. Incase of any difficulty encountered during the registration process, candidates are advised to contact the office of the Commission or email the Commission at their official email ID.

This issue with the approval of the competent authority.

Sd/-
Joint Director (IT),
