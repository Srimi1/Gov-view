(3) Rate of Recruitment Fees:-

Group-B Non- Gazetted posts:-Rs. 350/-(Rupees three hundred and fifty) for General
Candidates and Rs.250/-/-(Rupees two hundred & fifty) only for ST/SC/ BPL card
holders/Physically Handicapped Candidates.

b)Recruitment fee so deposited, is non-refundable.

(c) If a candidate submits incomplete application in respect of terms & condition of the
Advertisement and without requisite recruitment fee, his/her candidature will be

rejected.

(4) Decision of the Commission as to the eligibility or otherwise of a candidate at any

stage of the selection process shall be final.

(5) Assessment and evaluation of necessary documents done by the Commission shall

be final and shall not be open to serutiny by any external authority.

(6) The Examination venue shall be closed 10 minutes before the Scheduled
Commencement of the Examination and no candidate shall be allowed to enter

thereafter. No functionary has any Authority in this regard.
