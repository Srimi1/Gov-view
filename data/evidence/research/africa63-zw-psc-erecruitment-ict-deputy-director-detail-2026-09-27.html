Deputy Director, Information Communication Technology Development and Consultancy

Ministry of Information, Communication Technology and Postal and Courier Services
Harare, Zimbabwe
Application Deadline October 05, 2026
Sign in to Apply

Role Overview

Contribute to the formulation, implementation, and review of national ICT policies and strategies in line with the Ministry's mandate and national development aspiration; Develop, adapt and implement Information Communication Technologies, paying particular attention to technology transfer, value addition and import substitution; Establish vibrant ICT Software development strategies; Provide expert ICT assistance to Ministries, Departments and Agencies; Coordinate the evaluation for ICT projects and ICT project management services (systems design and development, user specifications documents); Manage software quality assurance; Manage research and regularly publish literate on ICT developments trends; Supervise, Coach and appraise subordinates

Requirements & Qualifications

  • A degree in Information Systems or Computer Science or Business Informatics or equivalent qualification
  • Relevant higher degree in an IT related field is an added advantage
  • Relevant IT related certification is an added advantage
  • At least six (6) years' experience on the Officer grade or equivalent level
  • Extensive knowledge of application systems development, support and maintenance
  • knowledge of network and hardware platforms
  • Exceptional ICT research skills
  • Strong oral and written communication skills
  • Ability to relate professionally at any level within and without the Ministry
  • Strong ICT problem solving skills
  • Strategic and analytic thinker
  • Conscientious character
