                 ANNOUNCEMENT FOR BHUTAN CIVIL SERVICE EXAMINATION (BCSE) 2026

The Royal Civil Service Commission is pleased to announce the overall schedule (Click here) for the Bhutan
Civil Service Examination (BCSE) 2026.
The details of the BCSE 2026 are as follows:
A)​ REGISTRATION PROCESS

   1.​ All the graduates wishing to appear for the BCSE, and join the Civil Service shall register online. For
       registration, visit the website at https://bcse.rcsc.gov.bt/Login

   2.​ The BCSE Online system shall remain open from June 8 to July 7, 2026.

   3.​ Graduates with PGDNL who aspire to join the Civil Service must register and appear for the
       Preliminary Examination (PE) and achieve the minimum pass percentage, and appear for the
       “Common Examination for Legal Officer” conducted by BAR Council of Bhutan to be eligible for
       selection and appointment to the Civil Service.

   4.​ B.Ed, medical (clinical and curative subject), and engineering professionals (who are exempted from
       PE) are required to register for the BCSE during the online registration phase.

   5.​ Until you receive an e-admit card, your registration is considered as “DRAFT”, and shall not be
       deemed SUBMITTED. Ensure you receive the e-admit card to confirm your registration.
   6.​ Registered candidates shall be provided with ALL necessary information on the venue, date & time of
       the PE on the RCSC website.

   7.​ A candidate shall be permitted to register for the BCSE provided they meet the age requirement of
       35 years. However, those existing in-service civil servants who have attained 35 years of age and
       above but not older than 45 years are required to fill out the form (Click here) and send it to
       bcse@rcsc.gov.bt along with a copy of Citizenship Identity Card to grant exceptional registration.
       Only upon receipt of the intimation from the RCSC, the registration shall be initiated.

   8.​ During registration, all the candidates are required to upload the following scanned documents in
       PDF format:
         ●​ Master's/Bachelor's degree Academic transcripts
         ●​ Class XII marksheet

B) EXAMINATION DETAILS

STAGE I: PRELIMINARY EXAMINATION (PE)
   1.​ The objective of the PE is to shortlist candidates for the Main Examination and ensure a minimum
       standard of the candidates.

   2.​ PE Papers shall be common to all categories of graduates.

   3.​ The PE shall be objective-type questions to test the following.
         ●​ English communication skills ​       ​       ​
         ●​ Dzongkha communication skills ​ ​
         ●​ Logic, analytical ability, and Problem-Solving
         ●​ Data interpretation ability​         ​
         ●​ General Awareness/Current Affairs ​          ​

   4.​ Only those candidates who achieve the minimum cut-off marks, which will be decided by the
       Commission, and those exempted from PE, shall be eligible to sit for the Main Examination (ME).

   5.​ Marks obtained in the PE shall not be carried forward to the Main Examination and shall be valid only
       for that particular year.

   6.​ An optical Mark Reader (OMR) answer sheet shall be used for answering the PE.

 STAGE II: MAIN EXAMINATION (ME)

   1.​ The objective of the ME is to test the candidate's core competencies and subject/general knowledge
       for the final selection.

   2.​ A candidate shall be permitted to appear for ME for an unlimited time, provided they meet the age
       requirement of 35 years for pre-service/45 years for all in-service existing civil servants candidates.

C) ACADEMIC REQUIREMENT
   1.​ Have a minimum of a Bachelor's Degree, a full-time on-campus course meeting the requirements of
       the minimum contact hours, and of at least three years duration from an Institute recognised by the
       competent authorities in any relevant field.

   2.​ Candidates from Shedras, who are awarded equivalent Bachelors/Masters Degree;

   3.​ With a minimum of a two-year Bachelors Degree acquired with a minimum of Class X and
       Certificate/Diploma of two years or more, if duly validated by the Royal University of Bhutan/Bhutan
       Accreditation Council/Bhutan Medical and Health Council and other similar authorities empowered
       by an Act of the Parliament.

   4.​ Meet the following subject requirements:
          a)​ Administrative Service: Minimum of a Bachelor's Degree in any field.
          b)​ Finance Service: Minimum of a Bachelor's Degree in Commerce/ Economics (Honours)/
              Economics (Majors)/Business Administration/Business Management.
          c)​ Education Service (PGDE): Minimum of a Bachelor's Degree/Masters Degree with at least one
              relevant subject prescribed for the Bhutanese School Syllabus or Higher Educational Institute
              Programme.
          d)​ Technical Service: Minimum of Bachelor's Degree in the relevant field of specialisation for the
              position announced, and a minimum of PGDNL for law graduates.
          e)​ Dzongkha: Minimum of a Bachelor's Degree in Dzongkha/Buddhist Studies.
          f)​ B.Ed.: Minimum of Bachelor of Education (B.Ed)

D) VENUE FOR PRELIMINARY EXAMINATION (PE)
   The RCSC will facilitate conducting of the PE in Mongar besides Thimphu for the convenience of the
   candidates. However, this option is subject to registration of a minimum required number of graduates
   opting to appear for the PE in Mongar. The RCSC shall announce on our website at least two weeks in
   advance whether the PE will be conducted in Mongar, as an alternative venue, or Not.

E)​ DOCUMENT VERIFICATION
   Candidates who qualify through the PE shall be required to report in person for verification and confirmation
   for the Main Examination in Thimphu as per the day-wise schedule, with the following documents in
   ORIGINAL.
      ●​   Valid Bhutanese Citizenship Identity Card;
      ●​   Academic transcript of semester-wise/year-wise or in the consolidated statement of marks;
      ●​   A graduate in the medical and health profession shall produce a certificate of registration/
           internship completion letter from the Bhutan Medical and Health Council.

   NOTE: From BCSE 2026 onwards, candidates are not required to bring any photocopies of the above
   documents. The verification team shall verify based on the original documents as per the uploaded
   document during registration.

F) SELECTION AND PLACEMENT

       1.​ The final selection and placement of the BCSE candidates into the Civil Service shall be based on the
           following:
             ●​ Obtained a minimum of 50% score in the Main Examination;
             ●​ Merit ranking of the Main Examination result of the category; and
             ●​ Availability of vacancies.


       2.​ In case there are tie-ranks in the final result of the Main Examination for a single vacancy or the last
           vacancy available, a candidate with the higher score in the Written Examination will be selected for
           the vacant position and subsequently by the Viva Voce score if there is a need to further break the
           tie in the Written Examination scores.

       3.​ An administrative fee of Nu. 5000 (Five Thousand only) shall be charged to those candidates who
           withdraw their selection between the signing of the confirmation and the oath-taking ceremony to
           cover the administrative cost incurred for conducting the selection and placement process again.

      1.​ Medical and Health profession graduates are required to complete their internship by 31st December
          2026 to be eligible to appear for the BCSE 2026.
      2.​ Certificate of Merit (if any) should be produced in the original and a complete set of photocopies
