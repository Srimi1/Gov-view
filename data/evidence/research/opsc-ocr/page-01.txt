2" Corrigendum to Advertisement No. 09 of 2026-27 for Recruitment
to the posts of Assistant Public Prosecutor

It is for information of all concerned that at the Para-7 of Short Advertisement
and Para-8 of the Long Advertisement No. 09 of 2026-27, the Date of Preliminary
Examination has been mentioned as 01.11.2026 (Sunday).

Keeping in view scheduling of OCS Main (Written) Examination-2025,
pursuant to Advt. No. 05 of 2025-26 from 29.10.2026 to 07.11.2026, the Date of
Preliminary Examination for the recruitment to the posts of Assistant Public
Prosecutor is here by rescheduled to 22.11.2026 (Sunday) vide Notice No.
5325/PSC dtd. 11.09.2026.

Hence, the Date of Preliminary Examination mentioned at Para-7 of
Short Advertisement and Para-8 of Long Advt. No. 09 of 2026-27 shall now be
read as 22.11.2026 (Sunday).

All other terms and conditions of the advertisement shall remain unchanged. |

at www.opsc.gov in for further information.
