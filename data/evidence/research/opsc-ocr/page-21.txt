2. VACANCY POSITION:

As per requisition furnished by the Home Department, Government of Odisha, out of
a total of 172 (58-w) vacancies, 86(29-w) vacancies are for UR category, 23(08-w)
vacancies are reserved for SEBC category, 27 (09-w) vacancies are reserved for SC
category and 36(12-w) vacancies are reserved for ST category.

Also, 08 posts are reserved for Person with Disabilities whose permanent disability is
40% or more, 09 posts are reserved for Ex-Servicemen and 02 posts are reserved for
Sports Persons

Candidates belonging to the categories of PWD, Ex-Servicemen & Sports Persons
shall be adjusted against the categories to which they belong

3.,AGE:

‘A candidate must have attained the age of 24(twenty-one) years and must not be
above the age of 42 (Forty-two) years on the 1° day of January, 2026 ie., he/she must
have been born not earlier than 2" January, 1984 and not later than 1% January, 2005.

4, EDUCATIONAL QUALIFICATION:
A candidate must possess the following educational qualifications:

(i) Accandidate must possess a Bachelor's Degree in Law from a recognised University;
(ji) He/She should have at least 02 (two) years of experience as practising Advocate.

5, EXAMINATION FEE:

The candidates other than SC, ST and PwD category shall have to pay the
examination fee amounting to Rs. 700/- (Rupees Seven hundred) only through online
mode while filling the Online Application Form. The examination fees paid shall be
refunded only to those candidates who actually appear in the Preliminary
Examination. Applications without payment of examination fees (except SC/ST/PwD
candidates) will be taken as incomplete and outrightly be rejected

Refund shall be issued in due course after completion of the preliminary
examination. Refunds will be processed to the original mode of payment used at the time of
the transaction by the candidate. (Credit Card, Debit Card, UPI, Wallet, etc.)
