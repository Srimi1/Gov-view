|. Preliminary Examination (100 Marks):

(i) The Preliminary Examination is only a screening test to shortlist candidates for
Main (Written) Examination.

(ii) It will consist of one paper of Objective Type Multiple Choice Questions (McQ
Pattern) with Optical Mark Recognition (OMR) Evaluation or Computer-based Test
(CBT) as decided by the Commission.

(iii) It will consist of 100 marks with 100 questions each carrying one mark.
(iv) For each correct answer, one mark will be awarded.

(v)_ There will be a negative marking. For each wrong response, 25% marks will be
deducted from the marks secured for correct answers.

(vi) For un-attempted questions, "zero" mark will be awarded.
(vil). The duration of the examination will be 2 (two) hours.

(viii) The qualifying marks will be decided by the Commission for each category of |
candidates.

(ix) The number of candidates who secure qualifying marks as fixed by the
Commission will be admitted to the Main (Written) Examination as per merit nearly as
possible but not more than 10 times of the total number of vacancies for each category
of candidates to be filled in the year through this examination.

(x) The marks secured in the Preliminary Examination will not be counted in
drawing the final Merit List for recommendation of appointment (Select List).

ll. Main Examination (650 Marks):

The Main Examination will consist of a Written Examination (600 Marks) and
the Personality Test/Interview (50 Marks). The marks secured by the candidates out of
the 650 marks will be counted in drawing the final Merit List for recommendation of
appointment (Select List).
