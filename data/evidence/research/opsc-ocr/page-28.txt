A. Main (written) examination:
(i) The Written Examination will be conducted in 4 (Four) Papers each carrying
150 Marks (Total 600 Marks).

(ii) The Paper will be Subjective and Descriptive Answer type.

(iii) The Commission will fix qualifying marks for each Paper and/or aggregate of
the Written Examination.

(iv) Candidates who secure qualifying marks will be shortlisted in the ratio of 1: 2
subject to a minimum of 5 (five) with reference to the number of vacancies in the
different categories to appear for the Personality Test/Interview.

B. Personality test/ interview:
(i) The Personality Test will carry 50 Marks.

C. Select list:

(i) The final Merit List for recommendation of appointment (Select List) will be
prepared based on the marks obtained by the candidates in the Written
Examination and Personality Test/interview taken together for different categories
equal to the total number of vacancies for each category of candidates to be filled in
the year through this examination.

(ii) If more than one candidate secures equal marks in the aggregate, the candidate |
securing higher marks in the Written Examination will be placed higher in the merit
list. In case of further tie, the order of merit shall be arranged in order of the date of
birth, i.e., the older in age will be placed higher in the merit list than the younger
ones.
