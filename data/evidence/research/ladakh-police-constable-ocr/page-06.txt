9. DESIRED QUALIFICATIONS

The following shall be treated as desired qualifications for award of incentive marks, subject
to the applicable rules:

a. Proficiency in driving and possession of a valid Light Motor. Vehicle Driving Licence with
transport endorsement; or

b. One-year Certificate Course in Computer Application from an institution recognised by
AICTE, with documentary proof of recognition.

A candidate may claim incentive marks for only one of the above desired qualifications.

The desired qualification test shall carry incentive marks up to 5% of the maximum marks of
the written examination,

For candidates claiming driving proficiency, a driving test shall be conducted in accordance
with the latest applicable driving-test guidelines of the competent department.

10. INCENTIVE FOR NCC CERTIFICATE HOLDERS

Candidates possessing valid NCC certificates shall be eligible for incentive marks as follows:

NCC Certificate Incentive
NCC “C” Certificate 5% of maximum marks
NCC “B” Certificate 3% of maximum marks
NCC “A” Certificate 2% of maximum marks

The prescribed certificate must have been acquired on or before the last date of submission
of online application forms.
