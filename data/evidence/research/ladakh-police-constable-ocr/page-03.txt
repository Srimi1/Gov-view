5. HORIZONTAL RESERVATION / SPECIAL CATEGORIES

In addition to the above vertical reservation, the following horizontal reservations shall be
applicable, subject to the applicable rules/orders:

1. 15% for serving Special Police Officers (SPOs) of Ladakh Police who fulfil the
prescribed educational, physical and medica! standards and have completed at least
three years of continuous engagement on the last date of submission of applications.

2. 4% for Voluntary Home Guards (VHGs) of Ladakh Police who fulfil the prescribed
educational, physical and medical standards and have completed at least five years of
continuous engagement on the last date of submission of applications.

3. 4% for Outstanding Sportspersons (OSP) who fulfil the prescribed educational,
physical and medical standards.

4. 20% for Ex-Agniveers who fulfil the prescribed educational, physical and medical
standards.

5. 6% for Ex-Servicemen, in accordance with the applicable rules/orders.

Where suitable candidates are not available against the respective horizontal reservation, the
vacancies shall be dealt with in accordance with the applicable Recruitment Rules/Standing
Order and shall not be carried forward, wherever so prescribed.

Special provision regarding Outstanding Sportspersons (OSP)

(i) 4% of the advertised vacancies shall be reserved horizontally for Outstanding
Sportspersons (OSP) in accordance with the Union Territory of Ladakh
Outstanding Sportspersons (Horizontal Reservation in Direct Recruitment to Group
'C' Posts) Rules, 2026, notified vide S.O. 247 dated 18.08.2026, and the applicable
instructions of the Department of Personnel and Training (DoPT), Government of
India.

(ii) OSP reservation shall operate as horizontal, interlocking reservation across the
applicable vertical reservation categories. A candidate selected against OSP
reservation shall be adjusted against the vertical reservation category to which the
candidate belongs.

(iii) For the purpose of this recruitment, an ‘Outstanding Sportsperson’ shall mean a
sportsperson eligible for recruitment in accordance with the eligibility criteria,
recognized competitions, recognized sports disciplines, order of preference and
other conditions prescribed under the aforesaid Rules and the applicable DoPT
instructions, as amended from time to time.

(iv) Only achievements in recognized competitions and recognized sports disciplines,
supported by certificates issued by the competent authority in the prescribed form,
shall be considered. The sporting achievement must have been attained on or
before the last date prescribed for submission of online applications, i.e.
41.09.2026.
