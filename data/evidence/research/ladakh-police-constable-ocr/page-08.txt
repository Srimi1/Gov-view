13. PHYSICAL ENDURANCE TEST (PET)
Only candidates qualifying the PST shall be permitted to appear in the PET.
Male Candidates

1, Long Race ~ 1600 metres in 7 minutes 30 seconds

2. Long Jump — 12 feet, three attempts

3. Push-ups — 20 (one cycle of up and down to be counted as one)

Female Candidates
1. Long Race — 1200 metres in 7 minutes 30 seconds
2. Long Jump —9 feet, three attempts
3. Shot Put—4 kg, 14.5 feet, three attempts
Note:

i. Candidates must qualify all the above tests separately.

ii. To qualify the long race, candidates must complete the distance within the
stipulated time frame as given above.

iii, Only those candidates who qualify the long race shail be eligible to attempt
the long jump, followed by push-ups for male candidates and shot put for
female candidates..

iv. The PST and PET are of qualifying nature only.

No candidate shall ordinarily be permitted to re-take a PET event after disqualification. Any
permissible appeal shall be dealt with strictly in accordance with the Standing Order and the
electronic/video/RFID record of the test.

14, COMMENCEMENT OF PST/PET
The PST/PET process shall commence from 22.09.2026 onwards.

The detailed schedule, batch-wise allocation, venue and reporting time shall be communicated
through Admit Cards and/or separate notices published on the official website of Ladakh
Police.
