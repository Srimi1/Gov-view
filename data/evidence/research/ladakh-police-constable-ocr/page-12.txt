28. EXAMINATION CENTRES
(i) The recruitment process shall be conducted at Leh and Kargil centres.

(ii) Candidates shall be required to indicate their preferred centre in the online application form.
Once the examination centre is allotted/selected, change of centre shall ordinarily not be
permitted after closure of the application process. .

29. APPLICATION PROCESS

(i) Eligible candidates shall submit their applications online through the designated recruitment
portal of Ladakh Police.

(ii) Candidates shall ensure that all particulars entered in the application form, particularly
name, date of birth, category, domicile and other eligibility particulars, are correct,

(iii) The candidate shall be responsible for the correctness of all information furnished.

(iv) After submission, the candidate should retain a printout/PDF copy of the application form
for future reference.

30. PREFERENCE FOR CADRE/UNIT

Candidates shall exercise, in the online application form, their order of preference for allocation
against the following cadre/unit groups, subject to their eligibility and availability of vacancies:

1. Executive / Armed /IRP / SDRF;
2. Border Battalion; and
3. Women Battalion.

(i) No separate preference shall be permitted or required within the group comprising
Executive / Armed / IRP / SDRF. These shall be treated as one preference group
and candidates shall not be required or permitted to indicate any inter se
preference amongst Executive, Armed, IRP and SDRF.

(ii) Only female candidates shail be eligible for consideration against vacancies in the
Women Battalion. Male candidates shall neither be permitted to exercise
preference for nor be considered against vacancies in the Women Battalion.

(iii) Only candidates possessing a valid Domicile Certificate establishing domicile in an
eligible border village, as recognized by the competent authority, shall be eligible
to exercise preference for and be considered against vacancies in the Border
Battalion.

(iv) A candidate shall exercise preference only for such cadre/unit group for which
he/she is eligible under the applicable rules and this Advertisement Notification.
Where a candidate is eligible for only one or two of the aforesaid preference
groups, the candidate shall exercise preference only amongst such eligible groups.

(v) The preference exercised by a candidate shail be taken into account for allocation
against available vacancies, subject to the candidate's merit, applicable
reservation provisions, eligibility, district-wise eligibility wherever applicable, and
the vacancy position. Mere exercise of preference shall not confer any right to
appointment against the preferred cadre/unit.

(vi) Allocation to the respective cadre/unit shall be made in accordance with merit,
applicable reservation, eligibility, availability of vacancies and the preference
exercised by the candidate.

(vil) The preference once submitted shall be treated as final and shall not ordinarily be
permitted to be altered after closure of the online application process.
