ii, Verification of educational, category, domicile, driving licence, OSP and other certificates
from the concerned issuing authorities. ©

No appointment shall be made unless the candidate is found medically fit and his/her character
and antecedents and certificates are verified satisfactorily.

22. PROVISIONAL NATURE OF APPOINTMENT

(i) Any appointment shall be provisional and subject to verification of the genuineness of all
certificates/documents furnished by the candidate.

(ii) If any certificate is found to be false, forged or otherwise invalid, the appointment shall be
liable to cancellation ab initio and appropriate legal action may also be initiated.

23. INSERVICE CANDIDATES

(i) Candidates already serving in Government service shall submit a No Objection Certificate
(NOC) from the concerned Head of Department along with the application, as prescribed.

(ii) Failure to furnish the required NOC shall render the candidature liable to rejection. .
24, LIABILITY TO SERVE ,

A person appointed to the service shall be liable to serve anywhere in the Union Territory
of Ladakh, or at such other place as may be specified by the Administration.

25. TRAINING EXPENSES

A person appointed to the service who resigns within the prescribed period shall be liable to
refund training expenses/pay and allowances as prescribed under the applicable rules/orders.

26. MISCONDUCT/MALPRACTICE

(i) If a candidate is found indulging in impersonation, concealment of facts, submission of false
documents, malpractice, misconduct or any other irregularity at any stage of the recruitment
process, his/her candidature shall be cancelled and the candidate may be debarred from
future examinations conducted by the Board for such period as may be decided under the
applicable rules.

(ii) Appropriate criminal/legal action may also be initiated wherever warranted.
27, ADMIT CARD

Candidates shall download their Admit Cards from the official! website of Ladakh Police and
shall carry the Admit Card, requisite photographs and prescribed original photo-ID proof at the
time of PST/PET and other examinations. In addition to the Admit Card, candidates
appearing for the Physical Standard Test (PST) must also bring two recent passport-size
colour photagraphs (fhe photograph should be the same as uploaded during submission
of the online application form) and one original ID proof. Acceptable Photo-ID proofs
include:

Aadhaar card/printout of e-Aadhaar,

Voter ID card,

Driving License,

PAN Card,

Passport,

College/University ID Card,

Employer’s ID Card (Govt./PSU/Private), etc.
