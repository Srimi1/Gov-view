6. DOMICILE

(i) A candidate applying for the advertised post must possess a valid Domicile Certificate of
the Union Territory of Ladakh, issued by the competent authority in accordance with the
applicable rules.

(ii) For the purpose of preference and allocation to the Border Battalion, the candidate must
possess a valid Domicile Certificate establishing domicile in an eligible border village, as
recognized by the competent authority.

7. ESSENTIAL EDUCATIONAL QUALIFICATION

(i) The candidate must have passed 10th Class/Matriculation from a recognised Board of
School Education.

(ii) However, serving Special Police Officers (SPOs) of Ladakh Police who have completed at
least three years of continuous engagement on the last date of submission of applications
shall be eligible with 8th pass qualification, subject to fulfilment of all other prescribed
conditions. :

(iii) The prescribed educational qualification must have been acquired on or before the last
date fixed for submission of online application forms.

(iv) Candidates whose qualifying examination result has been declared on or before the cut-
off date and who are declared passed may also be considered, subject to production of
documentary evidence.
