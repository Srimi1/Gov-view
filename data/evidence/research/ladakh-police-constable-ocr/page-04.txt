Candidates claiming OSP reservation shall indicate the claim in the online
application and shall furnish/upload the prescribed sports certificate(s) and
supporting documents in the manner specified by the Board. Where the recruitment
portal does not permit complete submission of the prescribed sports documents at
the initial stage, the Board may prescribe a separate OSP document-
submission/scrutiny window before preparation of the Sports Merit List.

The competent authorities for issuance of sports eligibility certificates and the
prescribed forms shall be thdse specified under the applicable DoPT instructions.
No certificate other than a certificate issued by the competent authority in the
prescribed form shall be accepted for determining eligibility under OSP reservation.
The Department of Youth Services and Sports, UT Ladakh, shall constitute a three-
member Committee of its officers for assisting the recruiting agency in examination
and verification of the certificates and sporting achievements submitted by
candidates. The recommendations of the Committee shall be taken into
consideration by the recruiting agency/appointing authority while determining OSP
eligibility.

Selection under OSP reservation shall be subject to verification of eligibility and
genuineness of certificates by the competent authority/recruiting agency.
Furnishing false, fabricated or invalid sports information/certificates shall render
the candidature or appointment liable to cancellation and may invite action ‘under
law.

Where an eligible OSP candidate is selected otherwise than against the OSP
reservation, such candidate shall not be adjusted against the OSP-reserved
vacancy; the next eligible candidate in the Sports Merit List shall be considered
against the OSP vacancy. If no eligible OSP candidate is available to fill the
prescribed reservation, the vacancy shall be filled in accordance with the normal
procedure prescribed for direct recruitment, as applicable.

In case of a tie in the Sports Merit List, preference shall be determined successively
by: (a) higher level of competition; (b) higher medal/position; (c) Senior level over
Junior level; (d) higher marks in the recruitment process, wherever applicable; and
(e) older candidate.

Where a candidate has participated in both Senior and Junior National
Championships/Games, the achievement at the Senior level shall rank higher, in
accordance with the OSP Rules, 2026.

Where a candidate possesses more than one sporting achievement, only the
highest achievement shail be taken into account for determining inter se merit. No
additional preference or weightage shail be granted merely for winning more than
one medal or securing more than-one position.
