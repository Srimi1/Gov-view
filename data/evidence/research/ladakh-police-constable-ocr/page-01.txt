Subject: Advertisement Notice for Direct Recruitment to the Post of Constable (Level-
02), General Cadre (Executive, Armed/IRP, SDRF, Women and Border Battalions) in
Ladakh Police — 2026. .

Online applications are invited from eligible candidates for direct recruitment to the post of
Constable (Level-02), General Cadre (Executive, Armed/IRP, SDRF, Women and Border
Battalions) in Ladakh Police, against 331 vacancies, in accordance with the Ladakh Police
(Subordinate) Service Recruitment (Revised) Rules, 2026, the applicable reservation
rules/orders, the Standing Order governing direct recruitment to the post of Constable (Level-
02), and other applicable orders/instructions issued by the Administration from time to time.

Link for Submission of Online Application:

Eligible candidates are required to fill out the online application form by visiting the official
website of Ladakh Police at the designated link, viz., https://police.ladakh.gov.in. The date of
activation of this link for submission-of-online application form shall be 29.08.2026, and the
last date for submission of the online application shall be 15.09.2026. Candidates may edit
their particulars in the online application form after the initial submission until the last date for
submission by logging in with their registered mobile number. However, the Date of Birth
and Category fields in the application form cannot be edited after submission.
Moreover, some fields may not be editable once submitted. Candidates are therefore
advised to fill in the form carefully and verify all details before submission. Candidates
are advised to visit the official Ladakh Police website regularly for updates.
