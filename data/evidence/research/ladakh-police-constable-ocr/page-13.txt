31. APPLICATION FEE

i) Fee Payable: 2600/- (Rupees Six Hundred) only for candidates belonging to
the Unreserved/EWS Category. For candidates belonging to SC, ST, and ALC
categories, the fee payable shall be %500/- (Rupees Five Hundrea) only.

ii) Mode of Payment: The fee can be paid only online via Net Banking,
Credit/Debit Cards, or UPI on the Recruitment Application Software (RAS) when
submitting the online application form.

iii) Non-Payment of Fee: Applications received without the prescribed fee shall
not be considered valid and will be summarily rejected. No representation
against such rejections shall be entertained. The fee once paid shall not be
refunded under any circumstances, nor shall it be adjusted against any other
examination or selection for any reason whatsoever. ,

iv) Refund of Excess Payment: All disputes related to the refund of excess
payment, if any, shall be governed by the Ladakh Police (Subordinate) Service
Recruitment (Revised) Rules, 2026. Candidates are advised to apply for a
refund of excess payment, if any, to the Board with documentary evidence.

32. TRANSPARENCY AND RECORDING OF RECRUITMENT

(i) The Ladakh Police Subordinate Service Recruitment Board shall endeavour to maintain a
fair, impartial, transparent, objective, technology-enabled and tamper-resistant recruitment
process.

(ii) The recruitment process shall be conducted on scientific lines and appropriate records of
PST, PET, document scrutiny and written examination shall be maintained for audit and
resolution of any representation/dispute.

33. RESIDUARY/INTERPRETATIVE PROVISIONS

(i) The Board may formulate procedural regulations, with the prior approval of the Head of
Police, for carrying out its functions under the applicable recruitment rules and Standing Order.

(ii) The Head of Police, UT Ladakh, shall be the competent authority for issuing amendments,
clarifications and interpretations in accordance with the applicable rules and Standing Order.

34, JURISDICTION

Any dispute relating to this recruitment shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the
Courts/Tribunals having jurisdiction over the Union Territory of Ladakh in accordance with law.

Before submission of online application form, candidates must carefully go through
the instructions given in this advertisement notice.
