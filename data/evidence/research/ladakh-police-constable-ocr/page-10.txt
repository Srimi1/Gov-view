18. INDICATIVE SYLLABUS

Subject Marks
General Knowledge 60
Reasoning 15
General Science 10
Arithmetic Abilities : 15
Total : . 100

General Knowledge: Current national and international affairs, India — History, Freedom
Movement, Art & Culture, Geography, Economics, General Polity and Constitution, Awards
and Honours, Sports, Gender and Weaker Sections, Environmental Issues, Common
Abbreviations, Current Technological Developments, and Ladakh — History, Art, Culture,
Geography and Current Affairs.

Reasoning: Analogies, similarities and differences, spatial visualisation, spatial orientation,
problem solving, analysis, judgment, decision making, visual memory, coding-decoding, non-
verbal series, number series, alphabet series and number ranking.

General Science: Basic principles of General Science and Life Science up to 10th Class level.

Arithmetic Abilities: Number System, Simple Interest, Average, Ratio and Proportion,
Percentage, Profit and Loss, Time and Work, Work and Wages, Time and Distance, Clocks
and Calendars, Mensuration, LCM/HCF, Simple Equations, Square Roots, Bar/Graphs and
Data Interpretation.

19. DECLARATION OF RESULT AND MERIT

(i) The list of provisionally selected candidates shall be prepared category-wise against the
advertised vacancies on the basis of cumulative merit in the Written Examination and
applicable incentive marks for Desired Qualification and NCC certificates, after applying the
applicable vertical and horizontal reservation provisions. Selection against the 4% OSP
reservation shall, however, be made strictly on the basis of the separate Sports Merit List
prepared in accordance with the OSP Rules, 2026.

(ii) The Board shall prepare the select list strictly in accordance with the Ladakh Police
(Subordinate) Service Recruitment (Revised) Rules, 2026, the applicable reservation
tules/orders, the Standing Order governing direct recruitment to Constable (Level-02), the
approved roster, the OSP Rules, 2026, and this Advertisement Notification. Allocation of
selected candidates to the respective cadre/unit shail thereafter be made on the basis of merit,
applicable reservation, eligibility, availability of vacancies and the preference exercised by the
candidate in the online application form

(iii) A waiting list may also be prepared in accordance with the applicable Standing Order.
20. TIE CASES

In case of a tie in aggregate marks, the following principles shall be applied successively:
i. Date of birth, with the older candidate placed higher in merit; and

ii, Alphabetical order of names.
