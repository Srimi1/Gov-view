11,Admit Card: - Admit card for any stage of examination will not be sent by post. Facility

for download of admits card will be provided on the website of Ladakh Police.

12. Exam Centre:-Ordinarily there will be two Centre for conduct of recruitment tests
(PET/PST & Written).One at District Leh & Kargil respectively. Once the Centre for
examination is selected / opted, in no circumstance the Centre can be change at any
later stage.

13. Resolution of Tie cases: In cases where more than one candidate secures equal

aggregates marks in overall marks aggregate tie will be resolved by applying the

following methods one after another till the tie is resolved:

i. Date of birth, with older candidates placed higher.

ii. Alphabetical order of the names of the candidates.

14. Declaration of Result: - The list of provisionally selected candidates, as per the
number of vacancies advertised, category wise from the list of candidates who appear
in the written examination shall be prepared by the Police Recruitment Board strictly on
