Subsequent to the declaration of final result of provisionally selected candidates- the

below mentioned formalities shall be carried out by respective controlling officers before

the Appointment orders are issued.

i. Medical Examination: The provisionally selected candidates shall be medically
examined by Board of Doctors in District Hospital Leh and Kargil as per the
prescribed standards in schedule [I-(A) of the Ladakh Police (Subordinate) Service
Recruitment (Revised)Rule 2026 and issue the certificate of health and medical
report as per the format annexed to this order as annexure |, Il & Ill.

ii. Verification of Character and Antecedents: Verification of character and antecedents
of the candidates will be conducted through concerned authorities expeditiously.
The senior superintendent of Police of each district will be responsible to liaise with

district authorities to expedite the verification.

iii, Documents Verification: -The verification of genuineness of educational qualification,
SC/ST/ALC/EWS/ Domicile/ Border certificate, driving license etc of all selected
candidates will be done from the issuing authorities by post or any other appropriate
means. The verification record of educational qualification, SC/ST/ALC/EWS
certificate /driving license etc. shall be kept with the service book of the candidate.

iv. Provisional appointment subject to verification: - Even where the prescribed

certificates have been produced, the appointing authority shall include a clause in
the offer of appointment as follows: -
"The appointment is provisional and is subject to the verification of the
Educational  Qualification/SC/ST/OBC/EWS/Domicile/Border _ certificate and
Driving License etc. If on verification, the certificates produced by the
appointees are proved fake or forged, the appointment shall be cancelled
abinitio. In addition, a criminal case may also be registered against the
individual as per rule & law".

V. The person appointed to the service is liable to serve anywhere in the UT of
Ladakh, otherwise, by any other specify orders of Government.

vi. Person appointed to service if resigned within three years of service has to
refund the sum to the government either all the training expenses incurred
upon the candidates or three months of pay and allowances received by
him/her prior to the resignation and per the other rule in position.

vil. In service candidate has to submit NOC from the concerned HOD along with
the application form.
