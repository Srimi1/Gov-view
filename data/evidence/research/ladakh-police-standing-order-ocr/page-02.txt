Since the post for Women & Border Bn are distributed amongst Districts (as per
LP(S)SR(R)R-2026), candidates belonging to the domicile District cannot apply for
appointment in another District. This should be supported by Domicile certificate in
term of SO 76 dated 27/06/2025 issued by UT Ladakh administration which shalt
determine the domicile district. In case candidates apply for appointment in more
than one district his application will be summarily rejected without any notice to the
candidates.
