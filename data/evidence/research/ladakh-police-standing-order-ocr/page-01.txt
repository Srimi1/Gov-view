2. Identification and notification of vacancies: - The AIG of Police (personnel)

will put up the vacancy position, category wise, to the Head of Police each year.

The Head of Police will take a decision regarding recruitment depending on the

number of vacancies and availability of training infrastructure. Based on above,

as the case may be the Head of Police will notify or may direct the Ladakh Police

(Subordinate) Service Recruitment Board to notify the vacancies for direct

recruitment to the post (s).

3. Transparent recruitment process: - To ensure a fair, impartial, transparent,
objective, temper proof, scientific, merit-based recruitment process so as to
induct into the Police individuals, best suited for their job, who have earned their
way into the police on their own merit and without favor or unfair means, the
Police recruitment board shall strive for the following objectives: -

i) To systemize recruitment procedures by using simple but well defined
procedures reinforced by use of technology and information
technology.

it) To keep the candidate informed of his progress at every stage of the
recruitment.

iii) To ensure that the whole process of PST, PET and other matters
concerning the examination process is video recorded to eliminate any
possibility of irregularity. Extensive videography should also be done
for the process of written examination.

iv) The Police Headquarters may appoint service provider for
outsourcing implementation of transparent recruitment process
through open tender under rule.
