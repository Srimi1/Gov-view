7. Essential Qualification: -

(i) Essential Educational Qualification: - 10" pass from recognized board of school
education (8th passed from any recognized school for serving SPO's who have completed
(03) years of continuous engagement on the last date of submission of application form).
The candidates will be required to produce marks sheet, provisional certificate etc. as proof
of having acquired the prescribed qualification on or before the cutoff date fixed for filling
online application form, failing which the candidature of such candidates shall be rejected
by the Board. The candidates who are able to prove by documentary evidence, that the result
of the qualifying examination was declared on or before the cut-off date and he/she has been
declared passed, will also be considered to meet the educational qualification.

(i) Age limit for the posts is 18-28 years; (age reckoning shall be as on the 1% day of
January of the year of recruitment)

(ii) Permissible relaxations in upper age limit for below mentioned categories, is in
accordance with the provisions of DoP&T OM No. 16667569393892 dated 06.10.2022.
and Home Ladakh No.A/573/2022-home section/2026/159-61 dated 2.4.2026.
