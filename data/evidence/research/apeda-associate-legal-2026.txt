      SUB: ENGAGEMENT OF ASSOCIATE (LEGAL) (ON CONTRACT) IN APEDA, NEW DELHI

The Agricultural and Processed Food Products Export Development Authority (APEDA), an
autonomous organization under the Department of Commerce, Ministry of Commerce &
Industry, Govt. of India is mandated with the responsibility for promotion of agricultural and
processed food products from India. APEDA has been actively involved in agricultural trade
facilitation and intends to improve market access for Indian agricultural products at
international level. The process involves improving quality of produce and achieving optimum
shelf life resulting into increased quality exports of Indian agriculture products. It also requires
addressing the issues of Sanitary & Phyto-Sanitary (SPS) and Technical Barriers to Trade (TBT)
which are currently resulting in poor market access and hampering exports. APEDA intends to
hire talented, innovative and dynamic professional(s) for the position of Associate (Legal) (On
Contract).

2.     Type and Tenure of Engagement

2.1    The engagement shall be at the level of Associate (Legal).

2.2     Scope of Work: The selected candidate will be deputed by APEDA who will work
        dedicatedly to ensure the following in collaboration with the concerned central/ state
        department/ agencies:

2.2.1 Liaise with law firms engaged to represent APEDA.

2.2.2 Be willing to learn and develop expertise in a niche area such as the law related to
      protection of geographical indications.

2.2.3 Be proactive and take initiative in handling the work expeditiously.

2.2.4 Prioritize the work and manage time efficiently.

2.2.5 Maintain strict confidentiality, given that the matters are highly sensitive.

2.3    The engagement shall be purely on a contractual basis.

2.4    The engagement shall be initially for a period of one year including probation period of
       three weeks. The tenure of engagement may be extended on a year to year basis,
       depending on the performance evaluation. After three years no further extension will be
       permissible under any circumstances.

       and experience would be considered for engagement as Associate (Legal).

 6.    Working Hours and Leave:

 6.1   Working Hours shall normally be from 9.00 AM to 5.30 PM office time during working

       Associate (Legal) may be required to sit late and may be required to attend office/work

 6.2   The Associate (Legal) will be eligible for 20 days leave during the period of one year, on
       pro-rata basis subject to the prior written approval of the controlling officer. Un-availed

       considered without remuneration with the prior approval of controlling officer.

       However, in rare and exceptional cases like need for professional development, training

       official exigencies.
 6.3   Apart from the above, the female Associate (Legal) may be eligible for maternity leave as
