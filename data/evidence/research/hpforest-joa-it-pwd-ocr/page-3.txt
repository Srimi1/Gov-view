incumbents/candidates of Lahaul & Spiti, Kinnaur & Pangi sub-division of Chamba District,
the last date of receiving applications is 10.10.2026. The applications received beyond this
period will not be entertained. The date of screening will be fixed by the Committee soon
after receipt of applications from the individual and will be intimated well in time

accordingly to the candidates.
