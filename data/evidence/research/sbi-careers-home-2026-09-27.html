DIVERSITY EQUALITY AND INCLUSION

DIVERSITY, EQUALITY, AND INCLUSION

Industry Best Benefits offered to our Employees that help our employees achieve their professional and personal goals.

WOMEN IN WORKFORCE

We lay strong emphasis on Gender diversity and take special care of our women employees who form 24.34% of our total workforce. Women employees can take up to 2-year sabbatical leave for personal commitments like childcare, education etc. We have a strong commitment towards protection of women and have a strict policy on prevention, prohibition, and redressal of sexual harassment at workplace.

EQUALITY IN RESPONSIBILITY

At SBI, there is fair distribution of roles and duties among employees, regardless of their gender, race, or other personal characteristics. It emphasizes ensuring that everyone within the organization has equal opportunities to take on responsibilities and contribute to the bank’s operations and goal. Each employee has a unique career path. You are initially groomed as an all-rounder, and thereafter you have the freedom to pursue your area of interest and build expertise.

INCLUSION OF COMMUNITIES

SBI promotes the inclusion of various communities in its workforce, this ensures representation of people from different background within the organization. SBI have specific policies or program targeting recruitment, training, and career development opportunities for individuals from marginalized or underrepresented groups as per Govt Policy. This enhances organizational diversity but also fosters a more inclusive work environment.

Motivational Session by Padmashri Mr. Sushil Doshi

Padmashri Mr. Sushil Doshi, eminent journalist, writer, and the first Hindi cricket commentator, delivered a motivational speech at the State Bank Bhavan Auditorium on 23rd September 2024. Launch of Historical Chronicle – Volume 5

The Bank enriched its legacy by launching the fifth volume of its historical chronicle, The Evolution of State Bank of India (1981–1996), along with its audio edition. The release was graced by Hon’ble Finance Minister and Minister of Corporate Affairs, Smt. Nirmala Sitharaman, who formally unveiled the volume. This milestone not only preserves SBI's rich heritage but also reflects the Bank’s continued commitment to documenting its remarkable journey and contributions to the nation’s economic growth.

Felicitation of Paralympics 2024 Medalists

A special event was organized to honor the outstanding achievements of Indian para-athletes at the Paris 2024 Paralympics. Held on 3rd December 2024, coinciding with the International Day of Persons with Disabilities, the event celebrated India’s best-ever Paralympic performance, with a remarkable tally of 29 medals — 7 Gold, 9 Silver, and 13 Bronze. This historic accomplishment not only highlighted the exceptional talent and determination of our para-athletes but also marked a proud milestone as India crossed the 50-medal mark in Paralympic history. To recognize their extraordinary contribution and bring glory to the nation, the medalists were felicitated by our top management in a memorable ceremony.

!!! CAUTION !!!

FAKE SELECTION LETTERS /LISTS SENT BY FRAUDSTERS IN THE NAME OF SBI

It has been reported to us that some fraudsters have hosted web sites making them appear to be as those of State Bank of India (SBI). Fake lists of selection for posts in SBI have been published on these websites, and reportedly fake appointment letters are also being issued in the name of SBI.

It is clarified that SBI never publishes names of shortlisted/selected candidates on the website. Only roll numbers/registration numbers are published and shortlisted/selected candidates are informed individually through SMS/Email/Post. All details regarding recruitment e.g. notice, interview schedule, final result etc., are published only on https://sbi.co.in/careers and https://bank.sbi/careers.

If any such communication is received, it should be cross verified for authenticity from the websites mentioned above
