Guidelines for selection and nomination of candidates Against Central Pool MBBS/BDS
seats for the academic year 2026-27

1. Selection of candidates by beneficiary States/Union Territories:

1.1 Beneficiary States/UTs:
The States/UTs, which are deficient in medical education.

1.2 Eligibility Conditions:
Only the children of:
1. Permanent residents of the State/UT concerned;
2. The employees of the State/UT Government concerned;
3. The employees of the Central/Other State/UT Government on deputation to the
State/UT concerned; and
4. The employees of the Central/other State/UT Govt. posted in and having their
headquarters within the State/UT concerned will be eligible.
The children of Central/State/UT Government employees aforementioned should be treated
at par with the local resident.
1.3 Education Qualification:
As per Graduate Medical Education Regulation of National Medical Commission, the
candidate must have passed in the subjects of Physics, Chemistry, Biology/Biotechnology,
and English individually and must have obtained a minimum:
+ 50% marks for Unreserved & Gen-EWS category
+ 40% marks for SC/ST/OBC category
For Person with Disabilities (PWD):
+ 45% marks for Unreserved & Gen-EWS category
+ 40% marks for SC/ST/OBC category

taken together in Physics, Chemistry, Biology/Biotechnology at the qualifying examination.

1.4 Selection of Candidates:

1.4.1 National Eligibility cum Entrance Test (NEET)
The selection of candidates will be made on the basis of rank obtained in the National
Eligibility cum Entrance Test (NEET)-2026 being conducted by National Testing Agency.
As per Graduate Medical Education Regulations of National Medical Commission, it shall be
necessary for the candidates to obtain minimum:

+ 50th percentile for Unreserved & Gen-EWS category

+ 40th percentile for SC/ST/OBC category
For Person with Disabilities (PwD):
+ 45th percentile for Unreserved & Gen-EWS category
+ 40th percentile for SC/ST/OBC category
The percentile shall be determined on the basis of highest marks secured in the All India
common merit list in National Eligibility Cum Entrance Test for admission to MBBS/BDS
Courses.

The reservation policy being followed by concerned beneficiary State/UT will apply on
Central Pool MBBS/BDS seats.

ANNEXURE:- A

Ladakh Central Pool Medical seats (selection of Candidates and Allotment of Colleges )
Guidelines 2021

Every academic year, the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, Government of India (GOT),
allocates general pool MBBS/BDS seats to various states and Union territories (UTs) including
UT of Ladakh under the deficient medical education category. The Government of India also
prescribes guidelines for allocation of these seats including the eligibility conditions, education
qualification, selection procedure and reservation etc. These guidelines have been formulated
to provide procedure to select the eligible candidates for the seats allocated to UT of Ladakh.

i. Short title, Commencement and Application

1) These guidelines may be called the Ladakh Central Pool Medical Seats (Selection
of Candidates and Allotment of Colleges) Amendment Guidelines, 2026.

2) They shall come into force with immediate effect.

3) These guidelines shall regulate the distribution of the central pool MBBS and BDS
seats, allocated to UT of Ladakh by the Government of India.

2. Definitions

i. Competent Authority means the Lieutenant Governor of Union territory of
Ladakh

ii. Eligible candidate means the person as defined under para-3 of these guidelines;

iii, Offer List shall be the list prepared and recommended by the Prescribed
Authority under these guidelines offering seats to the selected eligible
candidates in the allotted colleges.

iv. Prescribed Authority means a committee or agency notified by the
Administration of Union territory of Ladakh for distribution of central pool
seats, selection of candidates and allocation of colleges under these guidelines:

y. The select list shall be the list prepared by the Prescribed Authority by following
the procedure prescribed under these guidelines;

3. Eligibility of candidates

For becoming eligible for central pool medical seats the candidate must:

1) fulfil the minimum qualification as prescribed by Government of India, in the
applicable guidelines for the particular year for becoming eligible for central pool
medical seats under the category of states/UT deficient in medical education;

2) have appeared and acquired minimum prescribed marks under relevant category in
the National Eligibility Entrance Test (NEET) conducted by the National Testing
Authority (NTA) valid for the year of application;

3) beachild ofa permanent resident of the UT Ladakh or in case of children of other
categories prescribed in GOI guidelines (Central/State/UT/Other state employees
posted and having HQ in Ladakh) the candidate must have passed 10th and 12th

Page 1 of 4
class or equivalent board examinations from an institution located in Ladakh
recognized from a statutory Board/University;

Note: For the purpose of these guidelines a permanent resident means a person who
had/has been residing in Ladakh for at least 15 years.

4) not have already secured a seat in any medical institution for undergoing a degree
during the year.

Availability of Medical Seat allotted to UT of Ladakh for Distribution

Availability of medical seats in the particular year shall be as conveyed by the Gol
during the particular year.

Application Process:-

1) The Prescribed Authority, after taking approval of the competent authority, shall
invite applications against the central pool MBBS and BDS seats allocated to UT of
Ladakh as early as the number of seats is conveyed by the Government of India and by
making wide publicity through print and electronic media.

2) As far as practicable, efforts shall be made to invite online applications.

3) Preparation of merit lists: After the last date fixed for applying by the eligible
candidates, the Prescribed Authority shall prepare the merit lists of applicants based on
NEET results of the particular year.

Process of selection:-

1) The Prescribed Authority shall examine all the information and documents submitted
by the applicant and satisfy itself about its genuineness.

2) In case of any deficiency in the application or the documents, the Prescribed
Authority shall forthwith inform the candidate and allow clarifying and submitting
additional information if any within 24 hours.

3) After completing the scrutiny as prescribed above, the Prescribed Authority shall
select the eligible candidates as per NEET merit.

4) Besides, the Prescribed Authority shall also prepare a provisional waiting list as per
NEET merit.

5) After selection of the eligible applicants in the manner prescribed above, the
Prescribed Authority shall prepare a select list in order of merit based on their NEET
ranking and proceed for allotment of colleges amongst the selected candidates.

Process of Allotment of Colleges:-
1) Ranking of the select list shall be the basis for considering preference for allotment
of the college.
2) The preference of colleges given by the candidate, while submitting their
application, shall be considered as their preference for the allotment of college and
shall be considered strictly in order of merit of the select list.

3) While considering the allotment of colleges, the preference given by a Male
candidate for a Women exclusive college shall be treated as Null and Void and
preference only for the other remaining colleges shall be considered for allotment.

4) The allotment of the college shall be made in the following manner:

i. The first candidate in the select list shall be considered first for allotment of
college and shall be offered the college given as first valid preference in his/her
application;

ii. After allotting of college to the first candidate, the next candidate in the select
list shall be considered for allotment of college and if his/her first valid
preference is available the same shall be allotted to the candidate. If the seat of
the particular college has already been allotted to the earlier candidates in the
select list, the colleges next in the preference shall be considered. The process
shall continue till a vacant seat is allotted to the candidate;

ii. The similar process shall be followed for each candidate of the select list strictly
in order of merit.

iv. Ifa candidate has not given any preference or given less number of preferences
and his/her preference(s) have already been allotted before considering his/her

Offer and Admission Process and Operation of waiting lists:-

After approval of the Competent Authority, the selected candidate(s) shall be informed
by the Prescribed Authority by the fastest means of communication about the offer for
taking further action for admission in the allotted college.

As soon as an offer of the seats is made to the selected candidate he or she shall report
to concerned college immediately for completing the admission formalities within the
prescribed date mentioned in the offer letter.

In case the selected candidate does not report to the college or complete the required
formality within the stipulated period, the next candidate in the waiting list shall be
given an offer of the seat.

In case the candidate in the waiting list fails to report to the college or complete the
required formality within the stipulated period the vacant seat shall be offered to the
remaining candidates in the waiting list in order of merit first from the overall waiting
list of UT Ladakh.

The above process shall be operated for each vacant seat until all seats are filled or the
waiting list is exhausted.

Interpretation and Power to relax:-

1) Ifany question arises relating to the interpretation of these guidelines, it shall be referred
to the Competent Authority whose decision thereon shall be final.

2) The Competent Authority may relax any of the guidelines in case of non-availability of
a suitable candidate under the prescribed category to make eligible other applicant(s)
for the vacant seat(s) on the recommendation of the Prescribed Authority.
