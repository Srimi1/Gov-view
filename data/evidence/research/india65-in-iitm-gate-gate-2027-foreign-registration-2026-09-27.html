Important Instructions

1. During GATE 2027 registration, you can select and continue with your verified DigiLocker account, if you have one (NOTE: A verified DigiLocker account means, your profile is verified through
e-kyc. Simply creating a DigiLocker account does not make it a verified account).

2. If you encounter any data mismatch between DigiLocker and the GATE 2027 portal, please complete your e-kyc in DigiLocker and try again.

3. Alternatively, GATE aspirants have an option of registering through unverified digilocker account. They can select the default ID proof during application filling process. These candidates' applications will be scrutinized after the registration deadline for necessary documents.

4. It is recommended to use a mobile phone with atleast 5 MP camera to capture your facial image in a well-lit area.
