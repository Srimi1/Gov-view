NEW APPLICANTS

The NYS Per Diem Court Interpreter application is currently CLOSED.

We will begin accepting online applications again from November 16th - December 15th, 2026.

We appreciate your interest. Please visit our website Language Access & Court Interpreters | New York Courts for more information.

PREVIOUS APPLICANTS

Applications submitted in August-September 2026:

Written Exam:

  • Candidates will be invited to participate in the 11/1/26 - 11/22/26 assessment session (tentative).
  • Self-scheduling links will be sent via email in mid-October 2026.
  • Results will be directly emailed to the candidate within 24 Hours.

For reciprocity and exam waiver request, candidates will be contacted once a decision is rendered.

Oral Exam:

  • Candidates who pass the written exam will be invited to participate in the next oral assessment session - 1/10/27 - 1/31/27 (tentative).
  • Self-scheduling links will be sent via email in the beginning of January 2027.

Please note oral exam results take a minimum of 6 months to be reviewed and rated.

When sending inquiries to OLAPerDiemTest@nycourts.gov, always include your 7-digit applicant ID number and language.
