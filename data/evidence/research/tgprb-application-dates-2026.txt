POLICE RECRUITMENT-2026: ONLINE APPLICATIONS INVITED
        FROM 19 T H AUGUST TO 9 T h SEPTEMBER 2026

       With regard to the 4 Notifications issued on 29th July 2026 by Telangana State Level Police
Recruitment Board (TSLPRB) to fill up 7,437 Vacancies of various Posts in Police, SPF, Fire
Services and Prisons Departments, Applications are invited from desirous and eligible Candidates
through Online mode only in the prescribed proforma which will be made available on TSLPRB
Website (www.tgprb.in) from 8 a m on 19th August (Wednesday) onwards till 5 p m on 9th September
(Wednesday) 2026. Applications received through any other mode will not be accepted and will be
summarily rejected.

       Candidates have to pay the Fees as shown in the Table in the next page. The fee collected
from the Candidates is one-time for the whole end-to-end process and will be towards –
           ➢ Application Fee, Processing Charges etc.
           ➢ Preliminary Written Test for all the Applicants (One 3-hours Exam for PC Level and One
             3-hours Exam for SI Level) [No PWT for Technical Posts]
           ➢ PMT / PET (Physical Efficiency) for all the Applicants who Qualified in PWT (Events
             with RFID Bibs / Wrist Bands, Digital Height and other equipment)
           ➢ Trade Test / Driving Test for all the Applicants who qualified in PMT / PET for the Posts
             of SCT PCs (Mechanic / Driver)
           ➢ Final Written Examination for all the Applicants Qualified in PMT / PET (One 3-hours Exam
             for PC Level and Four 3-hours including two Descriptive Papers Exam for SI Level)
           ➢ Conducting Biometrics at various stages
           ➢ Portal Maintenance, Software Development and Result Processing Charges
           ➢ Other miscellaneous and contingent expenses

       There are 5 types of Common Procedure Regimens in the present Recruitment
Process as shown above with a single Physical Measurement Test / Physical Efficiency
Test (PMT / PET) to be conducted for the Posts / Notifications applied for by each
Candidate and the same PMT / PET readings will be used for all the Posts for which he /
she applied for. As indicated above, those Candidates who applied in more than 1 Common
Procedure Regimen will be afforded the benefit of saving on account of having a common
Physical Measurement Test / Physical Efficiency Test @ ₹100/- in case of OC / BC
Candidates per every additional Common Procedure Regimen applied for (besides one
Common Procedure Regimen) and @ ₹ 50/- in case of SC / ST Candidates.

       The details of the procedure for Payment of Fee, Instructions for Filling up of Part I
Online Application and General Instructions are already mentioned in the detailed
Notifications dated 29th July 2026 for the ready reference of the Candidates

       Candidates are requested to download the Aadhaar App on their Mobile Phones from
the Google Play Store or Apple App Store prior to filling up of the Part I Application/s for
Aadhaar Face Authentication.

       Candidates may contact Help Line Number shown on the Website (www.tgprb.in)
to clarify doubts, if any, while filling up of the Online Application/s. After submitting the
Online Application/s, Candidates have to take out a print-out of the finally submitted
Online Application/s for further reference. TSLPRB will not entertain any correspondence
from any Candidate directly.
