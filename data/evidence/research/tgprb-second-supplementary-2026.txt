AMENDMENT:
       The Government of Telangana vide GO Ms No. 129, General Administration (Services-A)
Department dated 09-09-2026 have raised the upper age limit prescribed for Direct Recruitment by
further 3 (three) years in addition to the 7 (seven) years already raised in G O Ms No. 87 dated 18-05-
2026, and G O Ms No. 122 dated 14-08-2026, General Administration (Services-A) Department in
respect of the Posts of Constables and equivalent Posts (except PC Driver in Police Department)
in Police, Fire Services, Prisons and Special Protection Force Departments. Therefore, this
relaxation shall be given in addition to the upper age limit mentioned in the Notifications.


       The above amendment affects the application of the following Paras of the 2 (Two)
Notifications issued on 29th July 2026 and Supplementary Notification issued on 15th August 2026 by
the Telangana State Level Police Recruitment Board.

 (1) Para No. 15-B (i), (ii) and (iii) of Notification in Rc No. 189 / Rect. / Rect.-2 / 2026, dated 29th
     July 2026 for the Posts of SCT PCs (Civil) and / or equivalent Posts
 (2) Para No. 14-B (i) of Notification in Rc No. 234 / Rect. / Rect.-3 / 2026, dated 29th July 2026 for
     the Post of SCT PC (Mechanic) and excluding the Post of SCT PC (Driver)
