About JAM

The Joint Admission Test for Masters (JAM) has set the benchmark for undergraduate science education in the country for the past two decades. This prestigious exam paves the way for students to gain admission to top-tier postgraduate programs, solidifying science as a stellar career choice nationwide. Our programs offer world-class education in various disciplines, designed to cultivate academic excellence and a fulfilling professional life.

What does JAM offer?

JAM is not just an exam—it's a gateway to premier institutes offering high-quality postgraduate education. The interdisciplinary nature of the curriculum empowers students to translate scientific knowledge into real-world applications. These opportunities are open to all eligible students, regardless of nationality, with English as the medium of instruction. With a JAM score, you can pursue a range of advanced degrees, including:

  • M.Sc.
  • M.Sc. (Tech.)
  • MS (Research)
  • M.Sc. - M.Tech. Dual Degree
  • Joint M.Sc. - Ph.D.
  • M.Sc. - Ph.D. Dual Degree
  • Integrated Ph.D. (I-PhD)

How Admissions Work

Admissions through JAM 2027 are categorized into two categories.

  • Admitting Institutes: Coordinated by JAM 2027 Organizing Institute.
  • Result Sharing Institutes: Candidates must approach these institutes directly after qualifying.

JAM 2027 Organizing Institute will coordinate admissions only for the Admitting Institutes. JAM 2027 qualified candidates can apply through the admission portal. For Result Sharing Institutes, candidates must apply directly to the respective institute.

Key Information for JAM 2027

  • Examination Date: February 14, 2027 (Sunday)
  • Mode: Computer-Based Test (CBT)
  • Subjects: BT, CY, EN, GG, MS, MA & PH
  • Examination Cities: Approximately 100 Cities
  • Eligibility: Open to all nationalities with no age restriction.

Admission Criteria

  • Admission is based on All India Rank (AIR).
  • Reservation policy of Government of India applies.
  • Foreign Nationals must follow institute regulations.
  • Qualifying degree must be completed before admission.
  • Documents must be submitted within institute deadlines.
  • Decision of JAM 2027 Organising Institute shall be final and binding on all the applicants.
