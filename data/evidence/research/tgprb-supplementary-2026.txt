                      Amendment to Notifications in

AMENDMENT:
       The Government of Telangana vide G O Ms No. 122, General Administration (Services-A)
Department dated 14-08-2026 have raised the upper age limit prescribed for the Direct Recruitment by
further 2 (two) years in addition to the raise of 5 (five) years issued in G O Ms No. 87, General
Administration (Services-A) Department dated 18-05-2026 in respect of various services / categories of
Posts of uniformed services in Police, Fire Services, Prisons and Special Protection Force Departments.
Therefore, this relaxation is in addition to the upper age limit mentioned in the Notifications.

       The above amendment affects the application of the following Paras of the 4 (Four)
Notifications issued on 29th July 2026 by the Telangana State Level Police Recruitment Board:
       (1) Para No. 15-B (i), (ii) and (iii) of Notification in Rc No. 189 / Rect. / Rect.-2 / 2026 dated
           29th July 2026 for the Posts of SCT PCs (Civil) and / or equivalent Posts
       (2) Para No. 15-B (i) of Notification in Rc No. 225 / Rect. / Rect.-1 / 2026 dated 29th July 2026
           for the Posts of SCT SIs (Civil) and / or equivalent Posts
       (3) Para No. 14-B (i) of Notification in Rc No. 234 / Rect. / Rect.-3 / 2026 dated 29th July 2026
           for the Posts of SCT PC (Mechanic) / SCT PC (Driver)
       (4) Para No. 14-B (i) of Notification in Rc No. 279 / Rect. / Rect.-3 / 2026 dated 29th July 2026
           for the Post of SCT ASI (FPB)
