Government Vacancies Enquiry System

Unless otherwise specified, all civil service jobs accept online applications only. Applications submitted by other means (including in person, by post, by fax or by e-mail) will not be accepted. The online application system will likely be busier towards the deadline of application and it may require longer time to complete your application. Applicants should allow sufficient time to submit applications via the means specified in the advertisements as early as possible to ensure timely completion of your application.

Applicants shall ensure that all information provided during the application process is true and accurate. If an applicant provides incorrect or false information during the application process, he/she will be disqualified for employment by the Government or be terminated (if already employed). The case will also be referred to law enforcement agency for follow up.

The G.F. 340 Online Application System allows applicants to save inputted information. Applicants can use the “Save Partially Completed Application to File” button to save the information inputted, which could be restored and uploaded to the System to continue with the application, or for applying for other jobs in the future (see Frequently Asked Questions 3 for details).

To safeguard personal data privacy, please do not submit application other than through the channel(s) specified in the recruitment advertisements.
