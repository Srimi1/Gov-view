(3) The candidates belonging to category "Children of Freedom Fighters”
shall be entitled to the benefit of above reservation subject to the following
conditions: -

(i) Benefit shall be admissible to one of the children only because the
Freedom Fighter himself being old-age is not eligible to get the benefit of
reservation, therefore, this benefit to one of his children in lieu thereof has
been allowed;

(ii) Benefit shall be admissible irrespective of the fact the Freedom fighter
himself is not present alive or not;

(iii) income criteria of the children shall not be taken into account for this
purpose;

(iv) where the benefit of reservation has already been availed by one or
more children of a Freedom Fighter in the selection list declared by
HPSC/HSSC or any other Recruiting Agency for appointment on regular
basis before the date of issue of these instructions, none of the
children/grandchildren of that freedom fighter shall now be eligible to get
the benefit again for any post(s) the result/selection list of which is
declared after the date of issue of these instructions.

(i) of re-advertisement of the posts twice to de-reserve the posts
