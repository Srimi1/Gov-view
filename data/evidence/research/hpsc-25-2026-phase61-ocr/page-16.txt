(a) acitizen of India, or

(b) a subject of Nepal, or

(c) asubject of Bhutan, or

(d) a Tibetan refugee who came over to India before1* January,1962 with
the intention of permanently settling in India, or

Lanka, East African countries of Kenya, Uganda, the United Republic of
Tanzania, Zambia, Malawi, Zaire, Ethiopia and Vietnam with the intention
of permanently settling in India.

Government of India.
