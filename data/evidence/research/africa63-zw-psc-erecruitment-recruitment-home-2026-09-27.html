How to Apply

  1. 1

    Register Account

    Create your PSC eRecruitment account with a few personal details.

  2. 2

    Complete Profile

    Add your qualifications, work experience, skills, and upload your CV.

  3. 3

    Search Vacancies

    Browse open positions and filter by location or category.

  4. 4

    Submit Application

    Apply to any vacancy you qualify for in just a few clicks.

  5. 5

    Track Application Status

    Follow your application's progress from your dashboard.
