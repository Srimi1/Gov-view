POLICE RECRUITMENT-2026: APPLICATION SUBMISSION DATE EXTENDED
                 LAST DATE IS 16TH SEPTEMBER, WEDNESDAY

        Telangana State Level Police Recruitment Board had issued 4 Notifications on 29th July
2026 for Direct Recruitment of 7,437 vacancies to various Posts in Police; Special Protection
Force; Fire, Disaster Response, Emergency & Civil Defence and Prisons & Correctional
Services Departments - aggregating to 275 Vacancies of SCT SI Civil and / or equivalent, 23
Vacancies of SCT ASI, Finger Print Bureau, 7,112 Vacancies of SCT PC Civil and / or
equivalent and 27 Vacancies of SCT Police Constable, Mechanic / Driver. Applications are
being accepted from the Candidates through ONLINE mode in the prescribed proforma that
has been made available on Website (www.tgprb.in) since 8 a m on 19th August 2026 for Direct
Recruitment to the Notified Posts.

        Besides, there are several perquisites including Medical Treatment to the Employee and
Dependent Family Members, Welfare Loans for House Building / Education of Children /
Marriages of Children, Risk Allowances (up to 50-60% of Salary in High-Risk Units),
Compensations for Job Related Injury / Damage, to which the Employees of Telangana State
Government are entitled to.

        At present, of the 3,38,336 Registered Candidates (including Women Candidates who
are almost 25% or ¼th of the total Candidates), 2,88,530 have proceeded forward after paying
the fee and of these 2,76,085 have submitted their Online Applications. These 2,76,085
Candidates have applied for 3,80,762 different Candidatures. Aadhaar Verification taken up
for the first time ever has already been completed for more than 95% of the Candidates.

        For the first time ever, Women have been provided ⅓rd or 33.3% reservation in all the
Posts notified for the Special Protection Force and the Fire Services Department. Further,
implementation of Group I, Group II and Group III reservations among SC Candidates is being
done for the first time in Police Recruitment, during this process. Additionally, offline Aadhaar
verification has also come into force in the 2026 Recruitment process.

        In view of the ongoing activity of filing Online Applications, the Chairman, Telangana
State Level Police Recruitment Board has recently participated in Live Programmes of
Interview-cum-Phone-in Interaction with the Candidates through T-SAT on 3rd September
2026 and All India Radio on 7th September 2026. During the Programmes, the Chairman has
clarified several doubts raised by the Candidates while noting their various concerns.

        In the circumstances aforementioned, with an intention to facilitate the Candidates to
have the opportunity to file their Online Applications conveniently it has been decided to extend
the last date of receipt of Applications till 5 p m on 16th September 2026, Wednesday. All
the aspiring Candidates – especially those who are Registered / who have paid Fee and are
yet to complete and submit their Online Applications are advised to submit their Online
Applications as early as possible because if they wait till the last day / last minute to submit the
Online Application they are most likely to face queuing congestion, delayed online response
and failed submission of Applications.
