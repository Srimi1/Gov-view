10), the number of posts shall be read as under:

 Sr.      Name of         Number                              Category
 No.    Department        of posts       UR      ST       SC        OBC         EWS
 10.    Directorate of       16           9        3       1         2            1

                       reserved for Persons with Benchmark Disabilities (PwD) (i.e
                       autism, intellectual disability, specified learning disability and
                       mental illness; and multiple disabilities from amongst persons with
