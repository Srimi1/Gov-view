4.0      JOB RESPONSIBILITIES, EXPERIENCE, ETC.:
4.1      Contractual Electrician
(a)      Job responsibilities of Contractual Electrician:
      1. Checking & minor repairing of electrical equipment such as motor, panel, wiring diagram and light
         fittings during testing, pre-commissioning & commissioning phases.
      2. Checking and minor repairing/re-connection of earthing system during testing, pre-commissioning
         & commissioning phases.
      3. Facilitating in operation of 2 x 1.4 MW Gas Genset Captive Power plant, 750 KVA Diesel Genset,
         250 KW load bank, Battery & UPS system during testing, pre-commissioning & commissioning
         phases.
      4. Other allied jobs as assigned by concerned reporting officer/HoD.
(b) Skills and knowledge required for Contractual Electrician:
      1. Experienced in testing of electrical equipment.
      2. Experience in checking insulation value of electrical equipment such as motor, transformers and
         cables.
      3. Operation and troubleshooting of DOL & Star delta motor starters.
      4. Cable termination, fixing of cable glands and assembling of NFLP & FLP light fittings.
4.2      Contractual Associate Engineer (Electrical)
(a)      Job responsibilities of Contractual Associate Engineer (Electrical):
      1. Facilitating in preliminary checking /testing of electrical equipment.
      2. Ensure quality control and strict compliance with safety standards during construction, installation
         and pre-commissioning phases of the electrical system.
      3. Supervise and ensure the safe operation of all electrical equipment during testing & pre-
         commissioning activities.
      4. Facilitating in troubleshooting of motor feeders, motor starters and equipment control wiring during
         testing, pre-commissioning & commissioning phases.
      5. Other allied jobs as assigned by concerned reporting officer/HoD.
(b)      Skills and knowledge required for Contractual Associate Engineer (Electrical):
      1. Experienced in testing of electrical equipment.
      2. Understanding operation of Star Delta & DOL starter of motors and its related wiring diagrams.
      3. Understanding Earthing system in the plant/ installations for body, neutral, lightning arrestor and
         instrument signal.
      4. Understanding of control diagram of air circuit breaker, motor feeders, power feeders, generator
         control of captive power plant and NGR system.

Note: The above list is only indicative and not exhaustive.\
