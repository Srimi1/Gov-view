Eligibility Criteria

Candidates who have completed their undergraduate degree or are appearing for the final examination of their qualifying degree in 2027 are eligible to appear for JAM 2027.

Who Can Apply?

Candidates who have completed their undergraduate degree or are appearing for the final examination of their qualifying degree in 2027 are eligible to appear for JAM 2027.

Admission through JAM 2027 is subject to the following eligibility conditions:

  • The undergraduate programme should have been COMPLETED before the date of admission of the respective Admitting Institute.
  • Proof of having passed the qualifying degree with the required eligibility, as specified by the respective Admitting Institute, must be submitted within the prescribed timeline.
