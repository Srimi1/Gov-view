Welcome to the Recruitment Portal

About the Portal

The Online Portal for Government Recruitment is to facilitate the conduct of recruitment by various departments of the Government of Puducherry by calling for online applications. This software is owned and managed by the Department of Personnel and Administrative Reforms (Exam Cell), Puducherry.

Features Available to Applicants

  • Read notifications/instructions
  • Register and apply online
  • Make modifications to submitted applications
  • View/download a copy of the online submitted application
  • View Messages sent from the recruiting department
  • View the status of the application
  • View and download the hall ticket
  • View the provisional selection list and marks secured by individual applicants
