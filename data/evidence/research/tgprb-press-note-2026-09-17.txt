         The State Government gave an age relaxation of 7 (5 + 2) years for SI / ASI level and SCT
 PC (Driver) Posts and 10 (5 + 2 + 3) years for PC level Posts (except PC Driver) over and above
 the maximum age limit prescribed in the Recruitment Rules for this process of recruitment because
 of which nearly 1.13 Lakh Candidates are estimated to have been benefitted.

         Telangana State Level Police Recruitment Board received Applications through Online
 mode in the prescribed proforma that has been made available on Website (www.tgprb.in) from
 19th August 2026 till 16th September 2026. During these 28 days of receiving Online Applications,
 the web-based Application has functioned most satisfactorily. All these 28 days, Phone-Helpline
 was made available from 10.30 am to 5.30 pm.

        With regard to the Aadhaar Face Authentication implemented for the first time in the Police
Recruitment to avert impersonation and to usher-in the state-of-the-art security, 97.4% of the
Candidates successfully completed their Aadhaar Face Authentication in the Aadhaar App on their
Mobile Phones during the filling of Part I Application Forms, and at the conclusion of the Online
Application Process,         only 2.6% of the Candidates could not complete the Aadhaar Face
Authentication. Candidates who could not complete Aadhaar Face Authentication have to
complete the process in their login area which will be made available till 30th September 2026
(Wednesday). After completion of the same, they can download their pdf Application Form/s.

        1,60,756* Applications have been received for the Posts of SCT SI Civil and / or equivalent
and 3,89,356* Applications have been received for the Posts of SCTPC Civil and / or equivalent
level. All the concerned Candidates in these 2 Testing Streams will have a 3-Tier Filtration Process
starting with Preliminary Written Test (PWT).

        11,992* Applications for the Post of SCT ASI FPB have been received. Similarly, 12,495*
Applications for the Post of SCT PC Driver and 2,465* Applications for the Post of SCT PC
Mechanic have been received. Candidates in these 3 Technical Testing Streams will have a 2-Tier
Filtration Process starting with Physical Measurement / Physical Efficiency Test (PMT / PET).

        Nearly 56.84% of the Applicants opted for Telugu as the Medium of Examination for the
Aptitude Test (wherever it is permissible to have the examination in Telugu or English or Urdu),
more than 43.02% opted for English and only 0.12% of the Candidates opted for Urdu.
