                             Walk-in-Interview for the award of Junior Research Fellowship (JRF)
DRDO Young Scientists’ Laboratory for Smart Materials (DYSL-SM) is dedicated to the development of smart material technology
solutions for national defence. The laboratory specialises in accelerated materials development, micro-robotics and stealth
technologies.
Indian Nationals are invited for walk-in-interview on 06.10.2026 @ 09:30 hrs for the position of One (01) Junior Research Fellow
(JRF) to work on project in the area of Development of Smart Materials based robotic application including shape memory alloy and
piezoelectric Materials.

General Conditions:

     1. The eligible candidates can “WALK IN FOR INTERVIEW” at DRDO Young Scientists Laboratory for Smart Materials (DYSL-SM), AMTL
        Campus (near DMRL), KANCHANBAGH, HYDERABAD-500058 on 06.10.2026 @ 09:30 hrs. Late comers will not be allowed.
     2. While appearing for interview, the candidates are required to bring all original certificates and submit the prescribed application form with
        recent passport size photograph affixed on top right corner of the first page and one set of self-attested copies of all Degrees/Academic
        qualification certificates/Mark Sheets/Experience Certificates etc.      Application Form can be downloaded from the DRDO website
        www.drdo.gov.in (DRDO website > Vacancies). Duly filled in application form with supporting documents in PDF format shall be
        submitted online on email ID: hrd.dysl-sm@gov.in (below 10MB).
     3. Screening of eligible applications will be based on GATE score, marks secured in degree, fulfilment of desirability criteria and relevant work
        experience (if any). The GATE score should be valid as on the date of interview in the aforementioned subject only.
     4. Based on the interview, a panel of candidates for the anticipated vacancy will be drawn which will be valid for a period of one year.
        However, empanelment doesn’t entitle any candidate to an offer of fellowship, which is contingent upon occurrence of vacancies during the
        year. Director, DYSL-SM reserves the right to not select any candidate, if suitable candidates are not available.
     5. DYSL-SM reserves the right to amend/modify or summarily cancel this advertisement. The above notified fellowships are likely to be
        increased or decreased or cancelled upon the prevailing requirement restrictions, if any, as per the policy of Government of India.
     6. No TADA will be paid for attending interview and for joining, if selected.
     7. Fellowship Emoluments: Rs. 37,000/~ per month for JRF with applicable H.R.A. and other emoluments as per DRDO rules.
     8. Tenure: The post is temporary in nature and the initial tenure is for 02 (Two) years. Subsequent upgradation to SRF grade is as per
        DRDO guidelines.
     9. Age Limit: The age limit for JRF is maximum of 28 years as on the date of application. The age limit shall be relaxed by 5 years for
        SC/ST and 3 years for OBC candidates or as per Govt. guidelines. A copy of self-attested caste certificate should be attached with the
        application.
     10. Only candidates who have completed the course and awarded degree/provisional certificate are eligible to apply for JRF position.
     11. The JRF candidates may be allowed to register for part-time Ph.D. (if selected) with any University/Institution during the tenure of fellowship
         upon approval granted by Director, DYSL-SM.
     12. The offer of fellowship does not confer on the candidates any right for absorption in DRDO under any circumstances.
     13. Those who were already awarded JRF position in any of the DRDO Laboratories/ centres earlier, are not eligible to apply again.
     14. Candidates working in Govt/Public sector undertaking / Autonomous bodies should produce no objection certificate.
