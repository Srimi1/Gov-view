Registration on the 'Kawader' Platform for Job Seekers

Civil Service and Government Development Bureau

•

Wednesday, January 29th, 2025

This service aims to register individuals as job seekers on the 'Kawader' platform, enabling them to explore and apply for available government job vacancies.

Incomplete Digital

1. Qatari ID card.

2. Academic certificate (with equivalency if obtained from outside the country).

3. Training certificates (if available).

4. Certificates of previous work experience (if available).

5. For job seekers who previously worked: Clearance certificate from the last employer in Qatar.

6. For persons with disabilities: Medical report from the Medical Rehabilitation Department at Hamad Medical Corporation and a membership card from the Qatar Society for Rehabilitation of Special Needs.

7. For male citizens who are not currently employed: Letter from the National Service Authority to complete employment procedures.

1. The individual must be between 18 and 60 years old.

2. The service is available for Qataris or children of Qatari mothers.

3. The required documents must be valid.

4. The educational qualification must be equivalent if obtained from outside Qatar.

5. The individual must not be retired.

6. The individual must not be currently enrolled in undergraduate studies.

7. The individual must not be a government scholarship recipient.

free

-

1 working day and 12 minutes
